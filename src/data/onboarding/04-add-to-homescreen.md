# Harvous works on any device

Harvous runs in a modern web browser, so you can use it on your laptop, tablet, or phone—whatever you’ve got. For a better experience on your tablet or phone, use your browser’s **share menu** and choose **“Add to Home Screen.”** Harvous then opens from an icon on your home screen and feels more like an app (no address bar, full screen).

Here are help articles for [iOS](https://support.apple.com/guide/shortcuts/add-a-shortcut-to-the-home-screen-apd735880972/ios) and [Android](https://support.google.com/android/answer/9450271?hl=en).
