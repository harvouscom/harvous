# Version 2.54.9

**Release Date**: August 13, 2026

## Fixes

- Allow selecting folders, Threads, highlights, resources, and search results, which offered Select but could not select anything
- Remove the Select option from the Scripture list, which had nothing to select
- Stop inserting a blank line after a quote placed partway through a note
- Stop note content duplicating between devices and returning after the duplicate is deleted
- Preserve a truncated draft's seam when a save conflict re-drafts it
- Open the passage itself from the "a passage you keep returning to" suggestion, instead of a list of notes
- Open a highlight with no source note in the standalone passage view, instead of doing nothing
- Hide the sermon heading on a note read through a space other than the one it was started in
