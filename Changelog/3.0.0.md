# Version 3.0.0

**Release Date**: September 4, 2026

The prototype shell replaces the classic one. See `release-notes/launches/harvous-3-0.md`
for the release story; this entry lists what the cutover carries.

Challenges shipped in this version and was withheld the same day — the reasoning, and the one
line that reverses it, are in `docs/future/CHALLENGES_AS_SUGGESTIONS.md`.

## Features

- Review — questions built from your own notes, verses, and the chapters you read, graded against the text
- Guest mode — write a note and highlight a verse before making an account, and keep the work on sign-up
- The Bible reader holds two translations at once, with offline translation packs
- Rename Home to Activity, the sidebar to Search, and Recall to Suggestions
- The Library panel replaces the command palette as the browse surface
- Chapters you read become review questions, and Activity offers a note about what you just read

## Improvements

- Price Plus at $7/mo and $49/yr; Founding is a first-year offer, not a lifetime-locked product
- Record who claimed the founding offer on `UserMetadata.foundingClaimedAt`
- An Activity load makes 31 requests, down from 42
- Review states read as plain words — still learning, you know this, slipping away

## Other Changes

- Retire the Review page; everything it held lives on Activity
- Retire `PrototypeSidebar`
- Challenges is not available while its design is settled
