# Version 2.43.0

**Release Date**: August 8, 2026

## Features

- A published study plan can be previewed publicly
- A room's next gathering shows to the people who gather in it
- A leader can see how many have opened each step of a plan
- A Scripture map of the books your church has taught from
- A room gets a front door to its own library shelf
