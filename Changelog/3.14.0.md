# Version 3.14.0

**Release Date**: September 26, 2026

## Features

- Churches can invite people with a link or a printable QR code
- Churches can write review questions for the people who follow them
- Organize a church's channels and groups into ministries
- Limit a channel to one ministry's groups or its leaders
- Schedule a channel post to go out at a set time
- Pastors can approve teachers' posts before they go out
- A Content list shows what is scheduled, waiting and published

## Fixes

- Teachers only write review questions for their own ministry
- Group leaders can open the plan they are able to edit
