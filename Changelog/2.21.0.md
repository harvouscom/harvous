# Version 2.21.0

**Release Date**: August 6, 2026

## Features

- Set your church's time zone and service times
- A teaching plan for each ministry
- Rename a sermon series once, everywhere
- Plan a whole series with Repeat weekly
- Give a volunteer leadership of one space
- See how many people your church reaches
- Resource Library for your own links and files

## Improvements

- Pastor and Teacher are now different roles
- Every church role has its own icon
- Teaching plan is now the Planner

## Fixes

- A lapsed church can still see its plan
- Removing a sermon frees its service times
