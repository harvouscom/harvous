# Version 2.61.2

**Release Date**: August 15, 2026

## Features

- Chapter addresses read as words, not percent signs
- The canon as a grid, one testament at a time
- The book picker opens where you are, under the canon's own headings
- Keep translations for reading without a connection
- The app remembers what was read, not only what was written
- A typed reference is a place you can go
- Read the canon as one book, and navigate from the title
- Margin bars that say which verses a note covers
- Group the sidebar's cards the way the church hub groups its lanes

## Fixes

- Two papers, and the parked note peeks from the bottom
- Read from the pack when the server can't answer, and stop the picker drifting
- A picker no longer slides away from the trigger it belongs to
- Settle on one name for Song of Solomon
- Chapter numbers pick as a grid, not a 40-row list
- Answer a name where you meet it, not in a list underneath
- One back button and one grip, wherever you meet them

## Other Changes

- The performance agent is the engineer
- Re-baseline after removing the passage-context lists
