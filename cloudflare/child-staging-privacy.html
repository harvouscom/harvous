Child notebook privacy preview

Harvous Staging is a separate test environment. Use a fictional child, a made-up nickname, and sample notes for this rehearsal.

What the rehearsal uses

Clerk handles the test parent sign-in. Epic Kids Web Services checks the adult’s email and returns a verification result. Harvous stores the consent record and sample notebook in the staging database.

What the parent can do

Use Family settings to read or export the sample notebook, end child sessions, withdraw consent, or delete the fictional account.

For operator contact details, see the Harvous Privacy Policy.
