# Using Spaces

Spaces are your main organizational containers in Harvous. Think of them as the top-level drawers in your digital filing cabinet.

## What are Spaces?

Spaces are the highest level of organization in Harvous. They contain threads and notes, and help you separate different aspects of your spiritual life:

- **"Bible Study"** - for your regular study notes
- **"Prayer Journal"** - for prayer requests and reflections
- **"Sermon Notes"** - for church service notes
- **"Personal Study"** - for your individual exploration
- **"Church Small Group"** - for shared group study

## Creating a Space

### Step-by-Step

1. **Click the "+" button** in the top navigation
2. **Select "New Space"** from the dropdown
3. **Enter a title** for your space
   - Make it descriptive and clear (e.g., "Daily Bible Reading", "Prayer Journal")
4. **Choose a color** from the 8 beautiful options:
   - Paper (default)
   - Blessed Blue
   - Mindful Mint
   - Graceful Gold
   - Pleasant Peach
   - Caring Coral
   - Peaceful Pink
   - Lovely Lavender
5. **Select privacy type**:
   - **Private**: Only you can see and edit
   - **Shared**: Invite others via share link; multiple people can view and contribute
6. **Click "Create Space"** to save

### Space Features

- **Color coding**: Each space gets a unique color for visual recognition
- **Item counts**: Shows total items (threads + notes) in each space
- **Persistent navigation**: Recently accessed spaces appear in your navigation sidebar
- **Paper color theme**: Spaces always use a clean paper color for consistency

## Private vs Shared Spaces

### Private Spaces

- **Personal study spaces** for focused individual work
- Keep your thoughts organized by major themes or study projects
- Perfect for personal Bible reading, prayer journaling, and individual reflection
- Only visible to you - your private spiritual journey

### Shared Spaces

- **Collaborative environments** where multiple people can contribute
- Perfect for church small groups, Bible study groups, book clubs, and family devotions
- The owner starts Threads and chooses the current Thread
- Members can view the current Thread, attach their own active notes to it, or compose a new note there
- **Invite via share link** — copy the link from Space details and share it; others join from the join page
- **Member management** — owner can see the people list and remove members; members can leave
- **Locked notes** are never shown in shared contexts (only you see your locked notes)

## Organizing Content in Spaces

Spaces can contain:
- **Threads**: Collections of related notes (e.g., "Gospel of John Study")
- **Individual Notes**: Notes that don't belong to any thread yet
- **Mixed content**: Both threads and notes can coexist in the same space

### Example Organization

```
📁 Bible Study Space (Private, Blue)
├── 📚 Gospel of John Thread (5 notes)
├── 📖 Romans Thread (12 notes)
└── 📝 Individual Reflection Note

📁 Prayer Journal Space (Private, Mint)
├── 🙏 Daily Prayers Thread (30 notes)
├── ❤️ Intercession List Thread (8 notes)
└── 📋 Prayer Request Note
```

## Managing Spaces

### Viewing a Space

- **Click on any space** in your dashboard or navigation to open it
- You'll see all threads and notes contained in that space
- The space header shows the color and total item count

### Closing a Space from Navigation

- **Hover over a space** in the navigation sidebar
- **Click the close button** (X) that appears
- **Confirm the action** - spaces can't be recovered once closed from navigation
- The space is removed from your navigation but still exists in your "Full List" view

**Important**: Closing a space from navigation doesn't delete it - it just removes it from your quick access navigation. You can always find it again in your "Full List" tab.

### Finding Spaces

- **Dashboard "Full List" tab**: See all your spaces organized
- **Search**: Search for space names to find them quickly
- **Navigation sidebar**: Recently accessed spaces appear here

## Best Practices

### Naming Your Spaces

- **Be descriptive**: "Bible Study" is better than "Study"
- **Use consistent patterns**: "Prayer Journal", "Sermon Notes", "Personal Study"
- **Keep it simple**: Don't create too many spaces - 3-5 main spaces is usually enough

### Organizing with Spaces

1. **Start with a few main spaces**: Don't create too many at once
2. **Group by purpose**: Separate personal study from group study
3. **Use colors strategically**: Choose colors that help you quickly identify spaces
4. **Let it grow**: Add new spaces as your study needs evolve

### Example Use Cases

**Daily Bible Reading**
- Create a "Daily Reading" space
- Add threads for different books or time periods
- Take notes each day as you read

**Sermon Notes**
- Create a "Sermon Notes" space
- Add threads for different series or time periods
- Take notes during services, then organize by thread

**Prayer Journal**
- Create a "Prayer Journal" space
- Add threads for different categories (requests, thanksgivings, intercessions)
- Date your notes and track how prayers are answered
