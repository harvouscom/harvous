# XP and Gamification

Learn about Harvous's XP (Experience Points) system and how it encourages regular Bible study and note-taking.

## What is XP?

XP (Experience Points) is Harvous's gamification system designed to encourage engagement and content creation. Think of it like a points system that rewards you for studying and taking notes.

### Why XP Matters

- **Motivation**: Encourages regular Bible study and note-taking
- **Progress Tracking**: See your spiritual study journey quantified
- **Engagement**: Makes the app more interactive and rewarding
- **Future Features**: XP will unlock levels, badges, and achievements

## How to Earn XP

### Creating Content

**Create a New Thread**: 10 XP
- Every time you create a new thread, you earn 10 XP
- Threads help organize your study, so they're rewarded

**Create a New Note**: 10 XP
- Every time you create a new note, you earn 10 XP
- Notes are the foundation of your study, so they're highly rewarded

### Daily Engagement

**Opening Notes/Threads**: 1 XP (up to 50 XP per day)
- Each time you open a note or thread, you earn 1 XP
- **Daily cap**: Limited to 50 XP per day to prevent gaming the system
- Encourages you to review and engage with your existing content

**First Note of the Day**: +5 XP bonus
- The first note you create each day earns an extra 5 XP bonus
- Encourages daily Bible study habits
- Total: 15 XP for your first note of the day (10 base + 5 bonus)

## XP Values Summary

| Action | XP Earned | Notes |
|--------|-----------|-------|
| Create a Thread | 10 XP | Always rewarded |
| Create a Note | 10 XP | Always rewarded |
| First Note of Day | +5 XP | Bonus on first note |
| Open Note/Thread | 1 XP | Up to 50 XP/day |

## Viewing Your XP

### Profile Page

1. **Click your profile** in the top navigation
2. **View your total XP** displayed with a bolt icon
3. **See your progress** as you create content

### Real-Time Updates

- XP updates in real-time as you create content
- No need to refresh - see your XP grow immediately
- Your XP reflects your engagement with the app

## Daily Bonuses and Caps

### Daily Engagement Cap

**50 XP per day** from opening notes/threads:
- Prevents gaming the system by rapidly opening/closing content
- Encourages genuine engagement without rewarding empty actions
- Resets each day at midnight

### First Note Bonus

**+5 XP bonus** for your first note each day:
- Encourages daily Bible study habits
- Rewards consistency over time
- Makes your first note of the day extra rewarding

## Why Daily Caps?

Without caps, users could "game" the system by rapidly opening and closing notes. The 50 XP daily cap encourages genuine engagement without rewarding empty actions.

### How It Works

- **First 50 opens**: Earn 1 XP each (50 XP total)
- **After 50 opens**: No more XP from opening content that day
- **Next day**: Cap resets, you can earn XP from opens again

## Understanding Your XP

### What XP Represents

- **Content Creation**: Your XP reflects how much content you've created
- **Engagement**: Your XP shows how actively you're studying
- **Consistency**: Regular study builds XP over time
- **Progress**: Watch your spiritual study journey grow

### XP and Your Study Journey

- **Higher XP**: More content created and more engagement
- **Lower XP**: Just getting started or less frequent use
- **Growing XP**: Active, consistent Bible study
- **Stable XP**: Maintaining your study habits

## Future XP Features

XP will unlock future features:

- **Levels**: Progress through levels based on total XP
- **Badges**: Earn badges for specific achievements
- **Achievements**: Unlock achievements for milestones
- **Leaderboards**: Compare progress with others (optional)
- **More Activity Types**: XP for sharing, commenting, etc.

## Tips for Earning XP

### Create Content Regularly

- **Daily notes**: Create at least one note each day for the bonus
- **Organize with threads**: Create threads to group related notes
- **Engage with content**: Open and review your existing notes

### Build Consistent Habits

- **Daily study**: Make Bible study a daily habit
- **Regular note-taking**: Take notes during study sessions
- **Review content**: Revisit your notes to reinforce learning

### Focus on Quality

- **Meaningful notes**: Create notes that help you grow
- **Organized content**: Use threads and spaces effectively
- **Regular review**: Engage with your existing content

## XP and Your Spiritual Growth

### Beyond the Points

While XP is fun and motivating, remember:

- **Content matters more**: Focus on meaningful study, not just points
- **Quality over quantity**: Better to have fewer meaningful notes than many empty ones
- **Growth is the goal**: XP tracks engagement, but spiritual growth is the real reward

### Using XP Wisely

- **Motivation tool**: Let XP encourage you to study regularly
- **Progress tracking**: Use XP to see your study habits over time
- **Not the goal**: Don't let XP become more important than actual study
