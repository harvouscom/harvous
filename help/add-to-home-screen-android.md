# Add Harvous to your home screen (Android)

Harvous works in Chrome and other mobile browsers. Adding it to your home screen opens Harvous full screen, like a native app, without the browser address bar.

## Steps in Chrome

1. Tap the menu (**⋮**) in the top corner
2. Tap **Install app** or **Add to Home Screen**
3. Confirm when prompted

Open Harvous from the new icon on your home screen or app drawer.

## Tips

- Chrome usually shows **Install app** when Harvous is installable
- Your notes stay synced — home screen and browser use the same account
- You can uninstall anytime like any other app
