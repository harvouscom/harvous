# Using Notes

Notes are your individual thoughts and insights in Harvous. They're the foundation of your Bible study - where you capture, reflect, and grow.

## What are Notes?

Notes are individual pieces of content - your thoughts, insights, reflections, and study notes:

- **"John 3:16 Reflection"** - Your thoughts on a verse
- **"Prayer for Healing"** - A prayer request
- **"Sermon Notes - Jan 15"** - Church service notes
- **"Cross-reference: Faith"** - Connecting different verses

## Creating a Note

### Step-by-Step

1. **Click the "+" button** in the top navigation
2. **Select "New Note"** from the dropdown
3. **Choose a thread** (or leave as "My Pile" for now)
   - You can organize it later if needed
4. **Add a title** that describes your note
   - Make it clear and searchable (e.g., "John 3:16 - God's Love")
5. **Write your content** using the rich text editor
   - Use the toolbar for formatting (bold, italic, lists)
   - Your content saves automatically as you type
6. **Click "Create Note"** to save

Your note will appear in the selected thread and be assigned a unique ID like N001.

## Understanding Note IDs

Every note gets a **sequential ID** like N001, N002, N003:

- **Never reused**: If you delete N003, the next note will be N004 (not N003)
- **User-friendly**: Easy to reference in conversations or study groups ("Check out my insight in N015")
- **Persistent**: IDs never change, even if you reorganize content
- **Sequential**: IDs are assigned in order as you create notes

### Why Sequential IDs?

- **Easy reference**: "See N042" is much clearer than "See note_1756318000001"
- **Conversation-friendly**: You can reference notes in study groups
- **Data integrity**: IDs help maintain order and prevent confusion

## Rich Text Editor

Harvous uses a modern rich text editor (Tiptap) with powerful formatting options:

### Formatting Options

- **Bold**: Make important text stand out
- **Italic**: Emphasize words or phrases
- **Underline**: Highlight key points
- **Lists**: Create ordered or unordered lists
- **Clean interface**: Distraction-free editing experience

### Using the Editor

1. **Select text** to format it
2. **Click formatting buttons** in the toolbar
3. **Type naturally** - formatting is applied as you go
4. **Content saves automatically** as you type

### Editor Features

- **Real-time saving**: Your content is saved automatically
- **Mobile-optimized**: Works great on phones and tablets
- **Consistent styling**: Uses the app's font family for a cohesive look
- **HTML content**: Supports rich formatting and structure

## Editing Notes

### Inline Editing

1. **Click on any note** to open it
2. **Click the edit button** or press `Cmd/Ctrl + Shift + E`
3. **Make your changes** in the editor
4. **Click "Save"** or press `Cmd/Ctrl + S` to save
5. **Press `Esc`** to cancel editing

### Editing Features

- **Inline editing**: Edit notes directly without separate edit pages
- **Context preservation**: See the note in its context while editing
- **Fast workflow**: One click to edit, one click to save
- **Auto-save**: Changes are saved immediately

## Adding Notes to Multiple Threads

One of Harvous's powerful features is that **notes can belong to multiple threads**:

### How It Works

1. **Create a note** in one thread
2. **Open the note** and click the "More" menu (three dots)
3. **Select "Edit"** or open the details panel
4. **Select additional threads** you want to add the note to
5. **Save your changes**

The note will now appear in all selected threads.

### Example Use Case

You create a note about "God's love" in your "Romans Study" thread. Later, you realize it also fits your "Personal Growth" thread. You can add it to both threads without creating a duplicate note.

## Note Preservation and Safety

### Notes Are Never Deleted

- **Always preserved**: Notes are never permanently deleted
- **Thread deletion**: When you delete a thread, its notes move to "My Pile" (not deleted)
- **Accident protection**: No fear of losing important insights
- **Complete history**: Every note you've ever created is saved

### How It Works

- **Delete a thread**: All its notes are moved to "My Pile" in "For You"
- **Remove from thread**: Removing a note from a thread doesn't delete it
- **Recovery**: You can always find notes in "My Pile" or by searching

### Why This Matters

Bible study insights are precious. A note you wrote months ago might become relevant again. We preserve everything so you never lose your spiritual journey.

## Note Content Tips

### Writing Effective Notes

1. **Clear titles**: Make titles descriptive and searchable
2. **Rich formatting**: Use bold, italic, and lists to structure your thoughts
3. **Scripture references**: Include Bible references in your notes
4. **Personal insights**: Capture your thoughts and reflections
5. **Questions**: Note questions that come up during study

### Example Note Structure

```
Title: John 3:16 - God's Love

Content:
**Key Verse**: "For God so loved the world..."

**My Reflection**:
This verse reminds me that God's love is:
- Unconditional
- Sacrificial
- For everyone

**Application**:
How can I show this kind of love to others?
```

## Finding Your Notes

### Search

- **Use the search bar** to find notes by title or content
- **Search by ID**: Type "N042" to find a specific note
- **Tag search**: Search includes tag matching for comprehensive results

### Navigation

- **Browse by thread**: Open a thread to see all its notes
- **Browse by space**: Open a space to see all notes (in threads and individually)
- **"For You" tab**: See unorganized notes that need sorting
