# Add Harvous to your home screen (iPhone & iPad)

Harvous works in Safari and other mobile browsers. Adding it to your home screen opens Harvous full screen, like a native app, without the browser address bar.

## Steps in Safari

1. Tap the **Share** icon (square with arrow) at the bottom of the screen
2. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**
3. Tap **Add**

Open Harvous from the new icon on your home screen.

## Tips

- Use Safari for the most reliable install flow on iPhone and iPad
- Your notes stay synced — home screen and browser use the same account
- You can remove the icon anytime from your home screen like any other app
