# Profile and Settings

Learn how to manage your Harvous account, customize your profile, and adjust settings.

## Viewing Your Profile

### Accessing Your Profile

1. **Click your profile** in the top navigation
2. **View your profile page** with:
   - Your name (First Name + Last Initial format, e.g., "John D")
   - Your avatar with initials (First Initial + Last Initial, e.g., "JD")
   - Your XP (Experience Points) with a bolt icon
   - Edit options for name and avatar color

### Profile Display

- **Name Format**: Shows "First Name + Last Initial" (e.g., "John D")
- **Avatar Format**: Shows "First Initial + Last Initial" (e.g., "JD")
- **XP Display**: Shows total XP with a bolt icon
- **Real-time Updates**: Profile updates immediately when you make changes

## Editing Your Profile

### Changing Your Name

1. **Click your profile** in the top navigation
2. **Click "Edit"** or open the edit panel
3. **Update your first name** and/or **last name**
4. **Save your changes**
5. **See updates immediately** across desktop and mobile

### Choosing Avatar Colors

1. **Click your profile** in the top navigation
2. **Click "Edit"** or open the edit panel
3. **Choose a color** from the 8 beautiful options:
   - **Paper** (default)
   - **Blessed Blue**
   - **Mindful Mint**
   - **Graceful Gold**
   - **Pleasant Peach**
   - **Caring Coral**
   - **Peaceful Pink**
   - **Lovely Lavender**
4. **Save your changes**
5. **See your new avatar color** immediately

**Note**: Avatar colors match the same palette used for spaces and threads, creating a cohesive visual experience.

## Troubleshooting Profile

### "My profile changes aren't saving"

1. **Check your connection**: Make sure you're connected to the internet
2. **Refresh the page**: Sometimes a refresh helps sync changes
3. **Try again**: Save your changes again
4. **Check other devices**: See if changes appear on other devices

### "I can't see my XP"

1. **Check your profile**: XP is displayed on your profile page
2. **Create content**: XP is earned by creating notes and threads
3. **Refresh the page**: Sometimes a refresh helps display updates
4. **Check your total**: XP accumulates over time

### "My avatar color isn't updating"

1. **Save your changes**: Make sure you clicked "Save" after choosing a color
2. **Refresh the page**: Sometimes a refresh helps display the new color
3. **Check other devices**: See if the color appears on other devices
4. **Try a different color**: Sometimes a different selection helps
