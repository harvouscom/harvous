# Tips and Best Practices

Advanced tips and strategies for getting the most out of Harvous.

## Organization Strategies

### Start Simple

- **Don't over-organize**: Start with 2-3 main spaces
- **Let it grow**: Add organization as your study needs evolve
- **Focus on content**: Better to have notes than perfect organization

### Use Consistent Naming

- **Be descriptive**: "Gospel of John Study" is better than "John"
- **Use patterns**: Similar content gets similar names
- **Include context**: "Sunday Sermons - January 2024" helps you find it later

### Regular Cleanup

- **Weekly review**: Set aside time to organize "For You"
- **Move content**: Assign notes to threads, threads to spaces
- **Don't overthink**: Sometimes "good enough" is better than perfect

## Study Workflow Examples

### Daily Bible Reading

1. Create a "Daily Reading" thread in your "Bible Study" space
2. Add notes for each day's reading (N001, N002, N003...)
3. Include: Scripture references, key insights, questions, applications
4. Review periodically to see patterns and growth

### Sermon Notes

1. Create a "Sermon Notes" space
2. Add threads for different series or time periods
3. Take notes during services, then organize by thread
4. Review later to reinforce what you learned

### Prayer Journal

1. Create a "Prayer Journal" space
2. Add threads for different categories (requests, thanksgivings, intercessions)
3. Date your notes and track how prayers are answered
4. Reflect on patterns in your prayer life

## Content Creation Tips

### Capture First, Organize Later

- **Quick capture**: Use "For You" for rapid note-taking
- **Don't worry about organization**: Focus on capturing insights
- **Organize later**: Move content when you have time

### Use Rich Formatting

- **Bold for emphasis**: Highlight key points
- **Lists for structure**: Organize thoughts with lists
- **Italic for quotes**: Distinguish quotes from your thoughts

### Include Context

- **Scripture references**: Always include Bible references
- **Dates**: Date your notes for chronological tracking
- **Questions**: Note questions that come up during study

## Productivity Tips

### Use Keyboard Shortcuts

- **Cmd/Ctrl + '** / **+ ;**: Create new note / new thread—**right hand** on **'** and **;** (next to each other above **Enter** on US QWERTY), **left hand** on the modifier
- **Cmd/Ctrl + F**: Search instantly
- **Cmd/Ctrl + E**: Edit notes fast
- **Esc**: Close panels quickly

### Leverage Search

- **Search by ID**: Find notes instantly by ID (e.g., N042)
- **Use keywords**: Search for biblical terms, book names, themes
- **Review results**: Most relevant results appear first

### Use Multiple Threads

- **Cross-reference**: Add notes to multiple threads when relevant
- **Flexible organization**: Content isn't locked into rigid categories
- **Natural connections**: Reflect how Bible study topics overlap

## Making the Most of Harvous

### Build Consistent Habits

- **Daily study**: Make Bible study a daily habit
- **Regular note-taking**: Take notes during study sessions
- **Review content**: Revisit your notes to reinforce learning

### Focus on Growth

- **Quality over quantity**: Better to have fewer meaningful notes
- **Spiritual growth**: Focus on growth, not just organization
- **Reflection**: Use notes to reflect on your spiritual journey

### Use XP Wisely

- **Motivation tool**: Let XP encourage regular study
- **Progress tracking**: Use XP to see your study habits
- **Not the goal**: Don't let XP become more important than study

## Advanced Organization

### Many-to-Many Relationships

- **Add to multiple threads**: Notes can belong to several threads
- **Cross-reference**: Link related concepts across different study areas
- **Flexible organization**: Content adapts to your needs

### Color Strategy

- **Choose meaningful colors**: Use colors that help you identify content
- **Consistent patterns**: Similar content gets similar colors
- **Visual organization**: Colors help you navigate quickly

### Space Organization

- **Separate by purpose**: Personal study vs. group study
- **Keep it simple**: 3-5 main spaces is usually enough
- **Let it evolve**: Add spaces as your study needs grow

## Platform-Specific Tips

### Mobile Tips

- **Quick capture**: Great for rapid note-taking on the go
- **Bottom sheets**: Get comfortable with slide-up panels
- **Touch gestures**: Swipe to dismiss, tap to interact

### Desktop Tips

- **Keyboard shortcuts**: Work faster with shortcuts
- **Sidebar navigation**: Keep frequently used items in navigation
- **Multi-column view**: Take advantage of additional column space

## Common Pitfalls to Avoid

### Over-Organizing

- **Don't create too many spaces**: Start simple
- **Don't overthink structure**: Let organization grow naturally
- **Focus on content**: Better to have notes than perfect organization

### Under-Organizing

- **Don't leave everything in "For You"**: Regular cleanup helps
- **Don't ignore organization**: Some structure helps you find content
- **Balance**: Find the right balance for your workflow

### Perfectionism

- **Good enough is good enough**: Don't overthink every decision
- **Reorganize when needed**: Nothing is permanent
- **Focus on study**: Organization serves your study, not the other way around
