# Getting Started with Harvous

Welcome to Harvous! This guide will help you get started with your Bible study notes journey.

## What is Harvous?

Harvous is a Bible study notes application designed specifically for organizing your spiritual study. Unlike generic note-taking apps, Harvous understands the unique needs of Bible study:

- **Flexible Organization**: Hierarchical system with Spaces → Threads → Notes
- **Rich Text Editing**: Modern editor with formatting options
- **Auto-Tagging**: Intelligent tagging with biblical keywords
- **Scripture Detection**: Automatic detection of Bible references
- **Multi-Thread Support**: Notes can belong to multiple threads
- **PWA Ready**: Installable on mobile and desktop

Think of Harvous like a digital filing cabinet specifically designed for Bible study:
- **Spaces** are like the main drawers (Bible Study, Prayer Journal, Sermon Notes)
- **Threads** are like folders within those drawers (Gospel of John, Romans Study, Daily Prayers)
- **Notes** are like individual documents in those folders (your thoughts, insights, and reflections)

## Signing In

1. Visit the Harvous app and sign in with your account
2. Your notes are private and secure - only you can see them
3. All your content is automatically saved and synced across devices

## Understanding the Interface

When you first open Harvous, you'll see:

### Dashboard

Your main workspace with two tabs:

- **"For You"**: Your inbox with unorganized notes and threads
  - Perfect for quick capture and later organization
  - Shows notes that aren't in any thread
  - Shows threads that aren't in any space

- **"Full List"**: All your organized content by spaces
  - Complete overview of your organization
  - Spaces with their contained threads and notes
  - Counts showing how many items are in each space/thread

### Navigation

- Quick access to your most recent spaces and threads
- Color coding: Each thread has its own unique color
- Counts: See how many notes are in each thread
- Close items: Hover over items to see a close button (removes from navigation)

### Create Buttons

The "+" button in the top navigation lets you:
- Create a new note
- Create a new thread
- Create a new space

### Profile

Click your profile to:
- View your XP (Experience Points)
- Edit your name and avatar color
- Access account settings

## Creating Your First Space

Spaces are your main organizational containers. Here's how to create one:

1. **Click the "+" button** in the top navigation
2. **Select "New Space"** from the dropdown
3. **Enter a title** for your space (e.g., "Bible Study", "Prayer Journal")
4. **Choose a color** from the 8 beautiful options
5. **Select privacy** (Private or Shared)
6. **Click "Create Space"** to save it
7. **You'll be redirected** to your new space where you can add threads and notes

**Space Features:**
- Spaces always use a clean paper color theme
- Once created, spaces appear in your persistent navigation when accessed
- You can close spaces from the navigation (with confirmation since they can't be recovered)
- Spaces show the total count of items (threads + notes) they contain

## Creating Your First Thread

Threads are collections of related notes within a space:

1. **Click the "+" button** in the top navigation
2. **Select "New Thread"** from the dropdown
3. **Choose a space** (or leave unorganized for now)
4. **Add a descriptive title** for your thread (e.g., "Gospel of John", "Romans Study")
5. **Choose a color** for your thread
6. **Click "Create Thread"** to save

Your thread will appear in the selected space with a unique color.

## Creating Your First Note

Notes are your individual thoughts and insights:

1. **Click the "+" button** in the top navigation
2. **Select "New Note"** from the dropdown
3. **Choose a thread** (or leave as "My Pile" for now)
4. **Add a title** that describes your note
5. **Write your content** using the rich text editor
   - Use the toolbar for formatting (bold, italic, lists)
   - Your content saves automatically as you type
6. **Click "Create Note"** to save

Your note will appear in the selected thread and be assigned a unique ID like N001.

## Understanding Note IDs

Every note gets a **sequential ID** like N001, N002, N003:
- **Never reused**: If you delete N003, the next note will be N004 (not N003)
- **User-friendly**: Easy to reference in conversations or study groups
- **Persistent**: IDs never change, even if you reorganize content

## Basic Navigation Tips

- **Click any note or thread** to open it
- **Use the navigation sidebar** to quickly access recently used items
- **Search** using the search bar to find any note or thread
- **Use keyboard shortcuts** for faster workflow (see [Keyboard Shortcuts](keyboard-shortcuts.md))

1. **Start Simple**: Don't try to organize everything at once
2. **Be Consistent**: Use similar naming patterns and organizational structures
3. **Regular Review**: Periodically review and organize your "For You" inbox
4. **Use the System**: Let the sequential IDs and color coding help you stay organized
5. **Focus on Content**: The organization is there to serve your study, not the other way around
