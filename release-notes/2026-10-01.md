# What's New in Harvous — October 1, 2026

**Release Date:** October 1, 2026
**Version:** v3.15.0

Harvous Plus now includes Connector: ask Claude, ChatGPT, or another AI app about your own Bible study, and it can read what you've written. It can't change anything.

---

## Updates in This Release

### Connector, included with Harvous Plus

**What changed:**
- Connect Claude, ChatGPT, or another AI app to Harvous from Settings, Connector. The page walks you through each app step by step, starting with a button that opens that app's own settings
- Ask about a passage, like "What have I written on Romans 8?", and it finds your notes on any part of it, along with your highlights and annotations from reading it
- Search your notes, read a note in full, and see the notes you've connected to each other
- In Claude, Harvous adds ready-made prompts to the + menu: what you've studied on a passage, preparing for your group, looking back on recent study, and tracing a theme
- In ChatGPT, deep research can draw on your notes and link back to each one in Harvous

**How it helps you:**
- Use what you've already studied in the place you're already asking questions
- Your notes stay yours: apps can read your study but can't add, edit, or delete anything, locked notes stay locked, and Scripture is shared as references only
- You can see which apps are connected and disconnect any of them in Settings

### Settings

**What changed:**
- "Plan & add-ons" is now just "Plan", and it lists Connector among what Plus includes

**How it helps you:**
- One plan, everything in it, nothing extra to buy

### Polish

**What changed:**
- A passage that runs across two chapters, like Exodus 6:28 to 7:7, now matches your notes on either chapter

**How it helps you:**
- "You've written on this before" finds those notes now too
