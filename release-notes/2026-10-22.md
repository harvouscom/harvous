## Updates in This Release

### Notes from the reader

**What changed:**
- When you're trying Harvous without an account, closing a note you had tucked below the chapter now takes you straight back to the chapter. Before, an empty note page could be left standing where the chapter should have been.

**How it helps you:**
- You keep reading where you were, and the note you started is still saved on this device.
