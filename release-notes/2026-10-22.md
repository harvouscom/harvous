# What's New in Harvous - October 22, 2026

**Release Date:** October 22, 2026
**Version:** v3.27.0

Search results now show what each one is and why it matched, and a note you set down above a chapter comes back when you tap it.

---

## Updates in This Release

### Search results that show why they matched

**What changed:**
- Every result in Library search now leads with a small tile showing what it is: a note, a highlight, a folder, a Thread, a passage or a verse. The note you have open stands out with a blue tile.
- A note's second line is now the sentence your search word appears in, instead of the note's first line.
- The words you searched for are underlined in the highlight color, in titles and in excerpts.
- When a note was last edited, or how many notes a folder holds, now sits at the right end of the title line.
- Each group of results shows how many it holds.
- The Library list you see before you type uses the same rows: notes, highlights, folders, Threads and books of the Bible each lead with their tile, and folders, Threads and books show their counts at the right instead of sitting in separate cards.

**How it helps you:**
- You can tell at a glance what each item is and pick the right one without opening it to check, and the Library looks the same whether you're browsing or searching.
- Seeing the sentence that matched tells you which note says what you remember, even when the word is deep inside a long note.

### Library lists laid out properly again

**What changed:**
- Notes and highlights in the Library panel had started showing their icon on a line of its own above the title, and the new search tiles weren't appearing. Both are back to looking the way they should.

### Notes over the Bible

**What changed:**
- When a note is set down above the chapter you are reading, tapping its title brings the note back up. Before, that tap could make the note disappear and leave a second copy of the chapter in its place.
- The × next to the title still puts the note away.
- When you're trying Harvous without an account, that × now takes you straight back to the chapter. Before, an empty note page could be left standing where the chapter should have been. The note you started is still saved on this device.

**How it helps you:**
- You can glance at the passage and get straight back to what you were writing, without hunting for the note again.

### Adding a verse to a Scripture reference

**What changed:**
- Backspacing into a chapter reference like Acts 21 and typing :8 now gives you Acts 21:8. Before, the verse could come out as "Acts 21 :8" and then disappear when you tapped away.
- A stray space around the colon, as in "Acts 21 :8", still counts as the verse you meant.
- If you start editing a reference and leave it unfinished, it goes back to exactly what it said before.
- Select All followed by Delete now clears a note even while you're in the middle of typing a reference.

**How it helps you:**
- References you add to stay the way you typed them, and you can always remove one.
