# What's New in Harvous — September 13, 2026

**Release Date:** September 13, 2026
**Versions:** v3.9.15–v3.10.1

---

## Updates in This Release

### New

**What changed:**
- Every note now keeps a history of earlier versions you can open and restore
- Your Activity feed now keeps the same history as your notes
- Review is now called Review exercises
- Discover now has six sermon-outline templates — Three-Point Sermon, HBLT, and four more shapes for building a message, credited to where they're taught

**How it helps you:**
- Open a note's ⋯ menu and choose History to see how it looked before, and bring an earlier version back. Free shows the last 90 days; Harvous Plus shows all of it.
- Activity follows the same rule, so older days stay reachable with Harvous Plus.
- Same feature, clearer name — nothing about it changed.
- If you're preparing to preach or teach, browse Discover for a ready-made outline instead of starting from a blank page.

### Fixed

**What changed:**
- Fixed a gap that could open up between an open study dock and the note or chapter behind it
- Fixed a bug where the Library panel's Threads tab could list a Thread that failed to open, showing "Could not load Thread."

**How it helps you:**
- The dock now sits flush on top of your reading again, the way it always has
- Every Thread in the list now opens.
