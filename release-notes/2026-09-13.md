# What's New in Harvous — September 13, 2026

**Release Date:** September 13, 2026
**Versions:** v3.9.15–v3.9.16

---

## Updates in This Release

### Fixed

**What changed:**
- Fixed a gap that could open up between an open study dock and the note or chapter behind it
- Fixed a bug where the Library panel's Threads tab could list a Thread that failed to open, showing "Could not load Thread."

**How it helps you:**
- The dock now sits flush on top of your reading again, the way it always has
- Every Thread in the list now opens.
