# What's New in Harvous — September 26, 2026

**Release Date:** September 26, 2026
**Versions:** v3.14.0

Churches can now invite people with a link, give them review questions to practise, organize their channels into ministries, and schedule or approve what goes out. The full story is in [Harvous for churches](launches/harvous-for-churches.md).

---

## Updates in This Release

### For people in a church

**What changed:**
- Scan your church's QR code or open its link to see the church, create an account, and follow its channels in one go
- Review questions your church writes show up in your Review, free
- Channels a church limits to one ministry only appear to the people in it

**How it helps you:**
- Getting connected to your church takes one scan instead of a search
- Practise what your church is teaching alongside your own study

### For church staff

**What changed:**
- An invite link and printable QR code, with a count of how many people joined through it
- Review questions: keep suggested passages or write multiple choice, ordering and matching questions
- Ministries: group channels and groups, and give a teacher just the ministry they lead
- Schedule a channel post for a set time, and turn on approval for teachers' posts
- A Content list for what is scheduled, waiting, sent back and recently published

**How it helps you:**
- The bulletin, the slides and the app finally point at the same place
- Teachers can prepare ahead, and pastors see everything before it goes out
