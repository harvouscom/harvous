# What's New in Harvous — October 30, 2026

**Release Date:** October 30, 2026
**Version:** v3.27.2

Churches can ask to join Harvous themselves now, and everyone connected to a church can choose the service they go to. The whole story is in [Harvous for churches](launches/harvous-for-churches.md).

---

## Updates in This Release

### Choose your service

**What changed:**
- If your church holds more than one service, choose yours in Settings › My Church, or from Home right after you connect
- This Sunday shows your service's time, and church notifications wait until it is over
- Channels and groups that meet during your service are listed first when you choose what to follow

**How it helps you:**
- Sunday shows the time that is actually yours
- Your phone stays quiet while you are in the service
- Finding the part of your church you belong to takes one look, and following is still your choice

### Bring your church to Harvous

**What changed:**
- Pastors and church staff can ask for their church from harvous.com: create an account, find the church in the directory, and say who you are
- We set the church up with you and start a 30-day pilot, with service times taken from the directory listing when it has them
- Anyone who had already picked that church in Settings is connected as soon as it is set up

**How it helps you:**
- Getting your church started no longer waits on an email thread
- Your congregation does not have to reconnect once the church arrives

### For church staff

**What changed:**
- Engagement shows how many people chose each service, once that is five or more, never who
- In Church settings, mark which channels and groups meet during each service
- The setup checklist ends with "Invite your congregation", and is ticked once someone joins through your link
- In a pilot's last week, the plan row says posting pauses when it ends
- When a plan has ended, scheduled posts wait instead of going out, and go out on renewal

**How it helps you:**
- You can see which services your people come to without seeing who they are
- A plan ending never sends half-finished posts out, or silently drops them

### Smaller fixes

**What changed:**
- Following a channel that has not posted yet now says so: "Following 1 channel · Nothing posted yet"
- Saving your church again from the Harvous app on iPhone or Mac no longer disconnects you from it or unfollows its channels

**How it helps you:**
- An empty feed explains itself instead of looking broken
- Your church connection stays put
