## Updates in This Release

### General improvements

**What changed:**
- Selecting several notes stops being a mode
- Highlights select, and the bar offers what a highlight can take
- Resources select, and only the ones that are yours to clear
- The selection checkbox stops charging every row for itself
- The selection orb stops crowding the title, and the actions say what they do
- The bulk bar gets states you can see, and its confirm comes to the button
- A bulk action opens on the notes you picked
- One reveal for every row, and cards say they are selected

**How it helps you:**
- Makes your Bible study workflow smoother
- Enhanced overall experience
- Smoother, more reliable experience

### Organization and structure

**What changed:**
- Every shared space gets a Planner, and a paired room plans beside its channel
- Folders and Threads select too, and the pattern gets a gallery scene
- A resource can go on to your church or into a space
- Thread is not offered to a single note

**How it helps you:**
- Better organization keeps your notes tidy
- Makes your Bible study workflow smoother
- Smoother, more reliable experience

### Visual polish
