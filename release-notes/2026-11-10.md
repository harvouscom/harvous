# What's New in Harvous — November 10, 2026

**Versions:** v3.32.5

### Editing notes on iPhone

If you use Harvous from your iPhone Home Screen, these updates make it easier to tap references, select text, and keep typing.

- Scripture references and suggested references open on a quick tap. You can scroll past them or press and hold to select text without opening them.
- Formatting controls stay available when you select text. You can make a phrase bold or italic even when the selection spans a Scripture reference.
- The editor gives the iPhone keyboard time to turn two spaces into a period after a typed Scripture reference, so you can keep typing from the right spot.
