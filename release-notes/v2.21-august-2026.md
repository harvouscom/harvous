# What's New in Harvous v2.21.0

**Release Date:** August 2026
**Version:** 2.21.0

Most of this release is for churches. There is one thing for everyone: a Resource Library for your own links and files.

---

## Updates in This Release

### A Resource Library of your own

**What changed:**
- Keep links and files in one place, alongside your notes
- This one is for everybody, not only churches

**How it helps you:**
- The article you meant to come back to has somewhere to live
- Your handouts and PDFs sit with the study they belong to, instead of in a downloads folder

### Your church's own clock

**What changed:**
- A church sets its time zone and lists the services it actually holds, like 9:00 and 10:45 on Sunday
- Service times show as the church's wall clock, never converted to yours

**How it helps you:**
- "This Sunday, 9:00 AM" means what your church means by it, even if you are reading three time zones away
- A church with a morning and an evening service can plan both, instead of choosing one

### A plan for each ministry

**What changed:**
- A ministry channel or church shared space can carry its own teaching plan
- Youth's Wednesday plan sits alongside the church's Sunday plan rather than competing with it

**How it helps you:**
- The person who runs a ministry can plan it without touching the main service
- What you see on Home is unchanged: your church's next service, and nothing else

### Series are real now

**What changed:**
- A series used to be a label typed onto each week. It is now a thing you can open, rename, and see the whole run of
- Renaming a series renames it everywhere it appears
- "Repeat weekly" plans a whole run at once instead of adding weeks one at a time

**How it helps you:**
- Renaming a series mid-run no longer quietly splits it in two
- Planning a quarter takes one pass, and if a week is already taken it stops there and tells you which one, rather than skipping it silently

### Volunteers can lead a space

**What changed:**
- Someone connected to your church can be given leadership of one space, from that space's own people list
- Leadership is granted deliberately and can be taken back the same way

**How it helps you:**
- The volunteer who actually runs the youth channel can publish to it, without being added to church staff
- It stays narrow on purpose: one space, and nothing else in the church

### Engagement, in counts only

**What changed:**
- A new Engagement pane shows how many people are connected to your church, and how many follow each channel

**How it helps you:**
- You can see whether a ministry is reaching people
- Harvous never shows a church who wrote what, or whether anyone wrote at all. Your notes are yours, and this pane does not change that

### Pastor and Teacher are different jobs

**What changed:**
- A pastor sets what the church teaches and what notes start from. A teacher reads the plan they teach from and publishes to channels
- The two roles previously granted exactly the same thing

**How it helps you:**
- A church can hand out the right amount of access instead of all of it

---

## Also in This Release

- "Teaching plan" in the church hub is now called "Planner"
- Removing a sermon now frees up the service times it was using. Those were being left behind, which quietly blocked that time slot on that date
