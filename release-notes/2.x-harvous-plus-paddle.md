# What's New in Harvous — Harvous Plus

**Release Date:** July 2026
**Version:** 2.x

Harvous Plus is the paid plan for hosting Shared Spaces, with billing handled securely through Paddle.

**Style:** Do not use emoji characters anywhere in release notes (titles, section headers, or body). Use plain words only.

---

## Updates in This Release

### Harvous Plus

**What changed:**
- One clear plan — Harvous Plus — for hosting Shared Spaces
- Founding price: $5 per month or $45 per year (annual shown first)
- Checkout stays on the upgrade page; manage subscription in a secure billing portal
- Joining a Shared Space remains free

**How it helps you:**
- You can create shared spaces and invite others to study together
- Tax and invoices are handled by Paddle as merchant of record
- Early subscribers keep the founding price when Plus grows

### Settings Plan page

**What changed:**
- Settings shows your Plan and what Plus includes
- Manage Subscription opens when Plus is active

**How it helps you:**
- You can see your plan status without hunting through billing drawers
