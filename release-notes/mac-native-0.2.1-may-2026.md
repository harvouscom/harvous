## Updates in This Release

### Editor

**What changed:**

- Scripture pill delete confirmation uses a compact single-row capsule (trash and dismiss) and anchors reliably when the pill is selected on iOS-style body layout.

**How it helps you:**

- Removing a pill stays visible and predictable instead of the confirm floating off-screen or showing an older two-button panel.
