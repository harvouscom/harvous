# What's New in Harvous — August 29, 2026

**Release Date:** August 29, 2026
**Versions:** v2.93.0–v2.97.1

---

## Updates in This Release

### Search remembers what you looked for

**What changed:**
- The search panel now keeps your recent searches and offers them back the next time you open
  it. Until now the panel threw the query away the moment it closed, so a search you ran in the
  morning was gone by the afternoon.
- A term is only remembered once the search has settled, so typing your way to "angels" does not
  leave "ange" and "angel" behind in the list.
- The field says what it is searching, rather than a bare magnifying glass.
- When nothing matches, the panel offers to start the thing you were looking for instead of just
  reporting that it does not exist.
- You can clear your search history from Settings, on the same row as the rest of your data
  controls.

**How it helps you:**
- The thread you were pulling on yesterday is one tap away instead of retyped from memory, and a
  search that finds nothing becomes the start of a note rather than a dead end.

### Selecting more than one thing at a time

**What changed:**
- You can now select several items in the search panel and act on all of them at once. Selecting
  is entered from the kind menu, in words, and while it is on, a bar above the list says how many
  you have chosen.
- It works across folders, Threads, highlights, Scripture and Resources, not just notes.
- In the Everything list you can mix kinds in one selection. The actions offered adjust to what
  you have picked, so an action that does not make sense for a folder is simply not offered, and
  anything that deletes tells you plainly what happens to each kind before it does it.
- Rows keep their place when selection turns on, rather than shifting sideways as the checkboxes
  appear.
- Escape now steps back one layer at a time instead of closing everything at once.

**How it helps you:**
- Filing a morning's worth of notes is one pass instead of one note at a time, and it works the
  same whether you are on a laptop or a phone.

### Activity

**What changed:**
- The day arrives as one thing, in sequence from below, rather than assembling itself a row at a
  time while you watch.
- Reloading the page paints the day you were last looking at instead of a screen of loading dots.
- A date jump lets you pick a past date from a calendar and go straight to it, keeping the stack
  you were reading.
- Each day is headed by its day of the week, rather than repeating the date twice.
- A day with nothing in it now reads as a quiet day rather than as a verdict on your study.
- A "What's new in Harvous" row appears after a release and links to the release notes. It puts
  itself away once you have read them, and does not come back until the next release.

**How it helps you:**
- Activity settles quickly and stays where you left it, and going back to what you were reading
  three weeks ago no longer means scrolling there.

### The account menu

**What changed:**
- The menu shows who you are signed in as, rather than only your initials.
- It no longer holds open space for things it is not showing.
- The filter box clears with our own clear mark instead of the browser's.

**How it helps you:**
- Fewer surprises about which account you are in, on a menu that is now the size of its contents.

### Dark mode

**What changed:**
- The panels on the Activity sheet, and the body of the search panel, were painting white in
  dark mode, which left the text on them almost unreadable. They now use the dark palette, and
  keep the same sense of one surface sitting on another that they have in light mode.

**How it helps you:**
- Activity and search are readable at night, which is when most of this gets used.

### Speed

**What changed:**
- Connection suggestions no longer hold up the rest of the page while they load.

**How it helps you:**
- The page is usable while the slower parts of it are still arriving.
