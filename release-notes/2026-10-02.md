# What's New in Harvous — October 2, 2026

**Release Date:** October 2, 2026
**Version:** v3.17.0

Pick up a conversation from Claude or ChatGPT in Harvous, and connect Grok and Muse too.

---

## Updates in This Release

### Start a note from a chat

**What changed:**
- Ask Claude, ChatGPT or another connected app to start a note in Harvous, or say yes when it offers at a natural pause, and it starts a new note for you
- The note opens with a card from that app: a short summary of what you talked about, the passages as tappable pills, and the question you were left with
- The page under the card is empty and yours to write in. The app's words stay in the card, and you can remove it any time
- The side panel shows which app a note was started in
- Apps ask before starting a note, can start at most 20 a day, and can never change or delete anything you have written
- It's off until you turn it on: Settings › Connector › "Let AI apps start notes"

**How it helps you:**
- A good conversation about a passage doesn't end in the chat. Carry it into your study and keep writing

### Connector

**What changed:**
- Ask your AI app "where was I?" and it finds the note you were working on and the chapter you were reading, the same as Home
- AI apps can now explore a passage's themes, cross-references, people and places, and list the notes in a series
- Settings › Connector now has setup steps for Grok and Muse alongside Claude and ChatGPT
- For Muse, copy a ready-made setup message and send it to Muse. It adds Harvous for you
- For Grok Bot, create a personal token to paste in. It is shown once, and you can replace or revoke it any time
- AI apps that register themselves with Harvous, like Muse, can now connect. Before, they could sign in but were then turned away on every request

**How it helps you:**
- Connect the AI app you already use, and pick up your study from wherever you left it

### Reading and notes

**What changed:**
- The strip above a note you opened from a chapter is easier to read. It no longer shows half-cut lines of the chapter behind it
- The strip names the verse you came from, like "Proverbs 8:24-25", and a note you set aside reads "Note on Proverbs 8:24-25" until you give it a title
- Flipping back to the chapter briefly fades everything but the verses you wrote about, so you can find your place
- A note started from selected verses now opens with those verses quoted under the passage pill. If the verses were highlighted, the quote takes the highlight's color, and any annotation you wrote opens the note
- After you type a scripture reference, a Quote button appears beside it. Tap it to add the verse text under it
- Fixed: a new note saved just after you flipped it down could appear on top of the chapter as well

**How it helps you:**
- See what you selected without tapping the pill, and find your way back to where you were reading
