# What's New in Harvous — August 10, 2026

**Release Date:** August 10, 2026
**Version:** v2.54.5

A fix for a rare case where reopening a note could show part of what you just wrote twice.

---

## Updates in This Release

### A note that just loaded won't repeat itself

**What changed:**

- If you started typing into a note before it had fully finished loading — or wrote in the installed app right before it got closed in the background — reopening it could sometimes show the part you just wrote twice in a row
- That's fixed: what you type now stays exactly what you typed

**How it helps you:**

- You can trust a note to hold exactly what you wrote, even if you started typing fast or got interrupted mid-edit
