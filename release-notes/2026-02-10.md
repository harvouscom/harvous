# What's New in Harvous — February 10, 2026

**Release Date:** February 10, 2026
**Versions:** v1.62.0–v1.64.0

**Shared Spaces v1 is complete.** This release marks the completion of collaborative shared spaces: invite members via email or shareable link, manage members, join and leave spaces, and tier limits (e.g. free: 1 shared space, 5 members). Going forward we'll focus on performance and quality-of-life improvements.

---

## Updates in This Release

- **Shared Spaces v1 complete:** Invitations (email + link), member management, accept/decline flow, leave space, tier limits
- **Join page & member view:** View shared space content as a member; locked notes never shown in shared contexts
- **UI:** Shared space indicator, SpaceMembersList, InviteMemberPanel, EditSpacePanel member section
- **Navigation:** Refinements and member sync on focus

---

- **Check tooltips:** Hover over buttons to see what they do
- **Visit /help:** Detailed guides for all features
