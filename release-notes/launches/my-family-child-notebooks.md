My Family started in October as a quiet way to study at home together: a shared Family Space, your Plus covering the rest of the household, and a progress view that never shows anyone's notes.

It was built for people 13 and older. Most families have someone younger.

This is the release that changes that. Children under 13 can now have a notebook of their own, set up and looked after by a parent, inside My Family. And when they turn 13, the notebook moves with them into their own Harvous account, so nothing they wrote is left behind.

---

## What's new

### A notebook for a child under 13

**What changed:**
- In My Family, **Invite someone** now offers **Child under 13**, beside Child 13 or older, Adult and Parent/Guardian.
- Instead of an invite link, you get **Set up their notebook**. You confirm you are an adult through Kids Web Services, our verification partner, read what Harvous keeps and why, and give the notebook your child's first name or a nickname.
- The notebook is listed under **People** in My Family, with the rest of your household.
- Each parent can look after up to six notebooks. They do not take any of your family's six places.

**How it helps you:**
- Your child gets a real notebook, not a cut-down toy. They can write notes, sort them into folders and Threads, read in My Bible, highlight and put stickers on verses, and read chapters together with you in a Family Bible.
- **Look back** brings back what they have read and written, a few questions at a time, and every answer is a tap. No typing, no grading their own notes.
- You stay in charge. You open the notebook for them with a sign-in link, choose which translations they can read, and can read, export, sign out or delete the notebook from My Family at any time.
- There is nothing in it that reaches outside your home: no public sharing, no messages, no purchases, no connectors.

Child notebooks are available in the United States.

### You choose how it starts out

**What changed:**
- Right after your child's name, you pick how their notebook looks the first time they open it: one of six colours, a pattern (Simple, Dots, Stripes or Checks) and a style (Classic, Spiral or Stitched).
- The cover always starts with your child's initial, and you see it change as you choose.

**How it helps you:**
- The first time your child opens Harvous, the notebook already looks like theirs.
- It is still theirs to change. Their cover studio has every colour, pattern and style, and the stickers are left for them to choose.

### At 13, it moves into their own account

**What changed:**
- When your child turns 13, open their notebook in My Family and choose **Move to their own account**. The page shows what moves and what stays behind.
- You confirm they are 13 or older and make a link. It works once, lasts seven days, and you can turn it off at any time.
- Your teen opens the link, signs in to their own Harvous account, and moves the notebook in.
- If your family has a free place, you can send a family invite along with it, so they stay in My Family as a member.

**What moves:**
- Their notes, folders, tags and Threads. Where they already have a tag or Thread with the same name, the two are joined.
- Their highlights, which appear in the Bible reader.
- Their Look back history, which becomes Review. Anything they had already been asked carries on from where it was.
- What they wrote in your Family Bible, as notes in a folder called Family Bible.

**What stays behind:**
- The notebook cover and its settings, and their verse stickers. All of these are in the backup you can download from My Family before the move.

**How it helps you:**
- Growing up does not mean starting over. Years of notes and highlights carry on in the account your teen will keep.
- The move is all or nothing. If anything goes wrong part-way, the notebook is left exactly as it was.
- After the move the notebook is theirs, and only theirs. The child notebook is closed, and you no longer have access to what they write.

## If you are new to My Family

My Family has never had a release note, so here is what it is.

- **A Family Space.** A shared space for your household to study in together.
- **Plus for the whole house.** If you have Harvous Plus and start a family, it covers up to five more people. They get Review, Challenges and their full history without paying for their own.
- **Progress, never notes.** A parent sees when someone was last active, how many chapters they read, which books and how many notes they wrote. Never what the notes say. Your child sees exactly the same view about themselves.
- **Our Bible.** The family's map shows which books of the Bible the household has been in, by name only. Everyone chooses whether their books are on it.

Child notebooks sit alongside all of this. They are looked after by the parent who set them up, and they do not count toward the six people in a family.

## Tips

### Setting up a notebook:
- **Quick tip:** Start a family first. Child notebooks are set up from inside My Family, under **Invite someone**.
- **Pro tip:** On a shared computer, use **Sign me out and open child notebook**, so your own account is signed out before your child starts writing.

### Moving a notebook at 13:
- **Quick tip:** Ask your teen to make their own Harvous account first, with an email they will keep.
- **Pro tip:** Download a backup of the notebook from My Family before the move, if you want to keep the cover and the parts that stay behind.
