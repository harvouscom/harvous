# What's New in Harvous — November 1, 2026

**Release Date:** November 1, 2026
**Version:** v3.29.3

---

## Updates in This Release

### Prompts that know when you go to church

**What changed:**
- The question that rotates above your notes on Activity now follows the service you chose in Settings, instead of assuming Sunday morning.

**How it helps you:**
- If you go to a Saturday vigil, a Sunday evening service or a midweek gathering, you are asked about the sermon when you have actually heard it, and about the week ahead the day before.

### Notes you write show up once

**What changed:**
- A note you keep open while you write it, like notes taken during a sermon, no longer appears on Activity a second time as "Returned to".

**How it helps you:**
- Your day reads as what you did: you wrote the note. "Returned to" is kept for when you actually come back to something later.
