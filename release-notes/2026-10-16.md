# What's New in Harvous - October 16, 2026

**Release Date:** October 16, 2026
**Versions:** v3.24.0

My Bible now has a group version. In a shared space, Our Bible shows where the room has been in Scripture, from the notes and highlights shared there. In a Family Space it shows which books the household has been in, from the people who choose to add theirs.

---

## Updates in This Release

### Our Bible

**What changed:**
- A shared space's Tools menu has a new row, Our Bible. It opens the same map as My Bible, drawn from the space instead of from you.
- A space's map counts the chapters its shared notes are about and the verses highlighted on them. A chapter that several notes come back to shows darker. Your own reading, and anything you keep in your own space, never appears on it.
- In a Family Space, Our Bible shows books only: never chapters, and never who. It starts empty. Each person chooses whether to add the books they've been in, and can take them back at any time.
- Invitations to a family now say this before you join.

**How it helps you:**
- A group can see what it has studied together, and where it has not been yet.
- A family can see the household's Bible fill in without anyone's reading being reported on.
