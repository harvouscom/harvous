# What's New in Harvous v1.216

**Release Date:** April 2026  
**Version:** 1.216.0 (see `package.json` for the current app version)

**v1.216.0** is a **minor** release focused on **space context you can trust**: opening threads and notes should match the space you are actually working in, including when the address bar carries an explicit space. It builds on April’s broader **v1.215.x** work; see [v1.215-april-2026.md](v1.215-april-2026.md) for the full month (navigation, Share Space, search polish, and more).

---

## Updates in This Release

### Space context and navigation

**What changed:**

- Thread and note links follow one clear rule for **which space you are in** when you open them: the space in the address bar when it is there, otherwise your current space from the route or space switcher. That reduces cases where a thread could feel like it opened “under” the wrong space.
- The main layout **respects the space in the URL** on thread and note pages, so headers and side content match the space you chose instead of snapping to the thread’s home space when those differ.
- Items on **My Home** can open with your **selected space** in the link so what you click lines up with the space shown in the switcher.
- **Mobile navigation** and **breadcrumb** shortcuts use the same logic so space context is preserved when you jump between notes and threads.

**How it helps you:**

- Spaces feel more predictable: the switcher, the URL, and what you just clicked stay aligned more often.
- You spend less mental energy wondering which space you are in after you open a thread or note from Home or the nav.

---

## Tips for Using New Features

- **Spaces:** If something still looks off after switching spaces, open the space from the switcher once, then open the thread or note again so the URL and context line up.
- **My Home:** Your selected space in the switcher can travel with links from the home list when it applies.
