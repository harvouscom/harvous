# What's New in Harvous — October 29, 2026

**Release Date:** October 29, 2026
**Version:** v3.27.2

---

## Updates in This Release

### Bug fixes

**What changed:**
- Activity rows on Home read cleanly again. A note that starts with a Scripture reference no longer runs into its next line, and highlights from your notes show the words you marked instead of a stray fragment.
- The time on each row stays whole on a phone, and the first row is no longer half-faded when you open the day.
- If your study can't load, Home now says so and offers Try again, instead of looking empty.
- Arrow keys in the Library panel or a menu no longer flip Activity to another day behind it.

**How it helps you:**
- Today's activity is easier to read at a glance, and a bad connection no longer looks like an empty day.
