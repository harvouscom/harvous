# What's New in Harvous — August 20, 2026

**Release Date:** August 20, 2026
**Versions:** v2.72.13–v2.72.16

A day of fixes to the Bible reader and to scripture references, nearly all of them found by using the app rather than by testing it. Nothing new to learn here — these are things that should already have worked.

---

## Updates in This Release

### A passage opens as a passage

**What changed:**

- Opening a reference that spans several verses lights all of them, not only the first
- Tapping a second verse extends the selection to cover both, so you can act on a passage on a phone
- Tapping inside a selection narrows it to that verse, and tapping the only selected verse clears it
- A reference that names a whole chapter opens the chapter without singling out verse one

**How it helps you:**

- Today's passage arrives as the passage it named — John 3:16-18 reads as three verses, rather than one verse and two you have to go and find
- Highlighting or noting a few verses no longer needs a keyboard, which is most of the time you are reading

### Margin notes keep up

**What changed:**

- Editing or deleting a note updates that chapter's margin marks straight away
- The chapter behind a note you are writing stays current while you write it

**How it helps you:**

- What is in the margin is what is actually there, without reloading to find out

### Reading details, whatever size the window is

**What changed:**

- Reading details open on a phone, where they previously did not appear at all
- In a narrow window the panel sits over the chapter and closes when you tap beside it
- Given room, the panel sits beside the chapter rather than over the verses you are adjusting it for

**How it helps you:**

- Changing translation or text size is available on the device you are most likely reading on
- You can always get back to the chapter, whatever size the window is

### References that stay where you put them

**What changed:**

- Writing a whole-chapter reference like "John 3" keeps its pill when the note saves
- Every reference in a sentence is marked, not only the first — "Psalm 23 and John 3:16" now gives you both
- A verse number no longer runs into the first word of its verse when a passage is quoted into a note
- The offline badge on a saved translation matches the other small labels in the app

**How it helps you:**

- A chapter you referenced stays linked to that chapter, instead of quietly turning back into plain text
- Quoted passages read properly, including ones already in your notes, the next time you change a pill

### Opening and starting notes

**What changed:**

- A note's folder, share and options are there as the note opens
- The toolbar holds its shape while a note loads, rather than shifting as each control arrives
- Starting a note from a template no longer redraws the page or rewraps the text a moment later
- The template list appears straight away instead of after a spinner

**How it helps you:**

- The controls are where you reach for them, rather than arriving after you have reached
- Choosing a template and starting to write is one continuous motion
