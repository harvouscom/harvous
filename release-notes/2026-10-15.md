# What's New in Harvous - October 15, 2026

**Release Date:** October 15, 2026
**Versions:** v3.22.1, v3.23.0

Two Catholic translations join the eleven you can already read: the New American Bible, Revised Edition and the Douay-Rheims. The Getting started checklist now notices what you do, wherever you do it. Review is simpler and stays in step with the rest of Harvous: two exercises are resting, a few cards are more compact, and the questions waiting for you are always current.

---

## Updates in This Release

### NABRE and Douay-Rheims

**What changed:**
- You can now read, compare and quote the New American Bible, Revised Edition (NABRE) and the Douay-Rheims Bible (DRB). Pick either one in the reader, as your default translation, or on a single scripture reference. Typing "DRA" after a reference works too.
- Both can be saved for offline reading, like the others.
- Verse numbers match the rest of Harvous. "Create in me a clean heart" is Psalm 51:10 in every translation, even though a printed NABRE numbers it 51:12 and a printed Douay-Rheims 50:12. Titles above the Psalms that the NABRE counts as verses are left out, as they are in the ESV.
- For now, both cover the 66 books of the Protestant canon. Tobit, Judith, Wisdom, Sirach, Baruch, 1 and 2 Maccabees, and the longer Greek parts of Daniel and Esther are coming in a later update.

**How it helps you:**
- If your parish reads the NABRE at Mass, or you love the older language of the Douay-Rheims, you can study in the Bible you already know.
- Highlights, comparisons and Review keep pointing at the same verse when you switch translations.

### Getting started

**What changed:**
- Each step checks itself off as soon as you do it, whether you started from the checklist or found your own way there. Open a chapter, highlight a verse, connect two notes, or answer a Review card, and the row ticks right away.
- More ways count: highlighting a verse inside a note, connecting notes from the Inspector or a suggested Thread, opening your Library from Search, and changing translation from the reader.
- Tapping "Revisit something" now opens the Suggestions tab, so you can see what it points to.

**How it helps you:**
- The checklist reflects what you have actually done, instead of asking you to repeat it.

### Two Review exercises are resting

**What changed:**
- The Takeaway question ("What did you take from this note?") no longer appears in Review, and its row is gone from your exercise settings.
- A question you miss no longer comes back a second time at the end of the same sitting.

**How it helps you:**
- Every question left in Review has a real answer to check you against. Takeaway could only ask you to grade yourself, and it will return once it can help you remember what you actually wrote.
- A sitting ends when you reach the end, without repeats. A question you missed still comes back on a later day.

### More compact Places and Theme cards

**What changed:**
- Cards that ask which place a verse names, or which theme it carries, now fit the verse instead of stretching it across a tall, mostly empty panel.

**How it helps you:**
- The verse and your choices sit close together, so there is less to scan and no empty space to look past.

### Review stays in step with the rest of Harvous

**What changed:**
- When you finish a sitting and go back to Home, Home no longer shows questions as still waiting.
- If you pause or remove a question somewhere else, or delete the note or highlight it came from, it drops out the next time you open Review.
- Changes to your exercise settings take effect right away, not from your next sitting.

**How it helps you:**
- What Home shows and what Review asks always match, so "done" means done.
- You are never asked about something you already set aside.
