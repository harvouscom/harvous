# What's New in Harvous - October 15, 2026

**Release Date:** October 15, 2026
**Version:** v3.22.1

The Getting started checklist now notices what you do, wherever you do it.

---

## Updates in This Release

### Getting started

**What changed:**
- Each step checks itself off as soon as you do it, whether you started from the checklist or found your own way there. Open a chapter, highlight a verse, connect two notes, or answer a Review card, and the row ticks right away.
- More ways count: highlighting a verse inside a note, connecting notes from the Inspector or a suggested Thread, opening your Library from Search, and changing translation from the reader.
- Tapping "Revisit something" now opens the Suggestions tab, so you can see what it points to.

**How it helps you:**
- The checklist reflects what you have actually done, instead of asking you to repeat it.

### Review

**What changed:**
- "Not now" on the Review sample now hides it.

**How it helps you:**
- When you put the sample away, it stays away.
