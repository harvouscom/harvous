# What's New in Harvous — October 15, 2026

**Release Date:** October 15, 2026
**Version:** v3.22.1

---

## Updates in This Release

### Scripture in your notes

**What changed:**
- The verse preview above a reference you are typing is now a slim, quieter strip. The translation sits in a small badge, and a count shows how many of your other notes cite the same passage.
- The quote button comes back when you change a reference. Edit Exodus 5:1 into 5:2 and you can drop the new verse into your note, the same as when you first typed it.

**How it helps you:**
- You can see what a reference says without the preview getting in the way of your writing.
- Fixing a reference no longer means deleting the pill and typing it again just to quote it.

### Review

**What changed:**
- Choosing Not now on the Review sample card hides it.

**How it helps you:**
- The sample stays out of your way once you have seen it.
