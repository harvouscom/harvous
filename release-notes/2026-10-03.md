# What's New in Harvous — October 3, 2026

**Release Date:** October 3, 2026
**Version:** v3.17.2

---

## Updates in This Release

### Scripture

**What changed:**
- Words that ran together in some verses now have their spaces back, for example "released and prisoners" instead of "releasedand prisoners". This affected poetry and lists in NIV, NLT, ESV and The Message, and a few verses in other translations.
- Bibles you saved for offline reading refresh themselves with the corrected text.

**How it helps you:**
- Verses read the way the translation prints them, in the reader, in verse popovers and in Today's passage.
