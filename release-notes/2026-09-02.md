# What's New in Harvous — September 2, 2026

**Release Date:** September 2, 2026
**Versions:** v2.129.0 - v2.132.0

Review now fills itself from your own study. Harvous keeps track of what you have been reading, marking, linking and writing about, and brings a few of those things back each day.

---

## Updates in This Release

### Review finds things for you

**What changed:**
- Harvous now keeps a record of what you study: the chapters you read, the verses you highlight and cite, the notes you come back to, the links you draw, and the Threads that grow out of them.
- Review reads that record and adds a few questions a day, never more than three. You do not have to add anything yourself, and there is no button to press to get started.
- Every row says where it came from. "Highlighted while reading John 15." "You linked these notes." "Cited in Covenant and kingship."
- The questions are mixed. A verse you marked, a note you keep returning to, a link you made, a Thread that has been forming — never three of the same kind in one sitting.
- Nothing you have seen in the last day is asked about, and nothing already scheduled is asked twice.

**How it helps you:**
- The more you study, the better the suggestions get, and they get better in ways you can trace back to something you actually did.
- A verse you highlighted last week can come back before a note you wrote two years ago, because how recently something mattered to you counts.
- You can always see why a question is there, which means you can trust it or dismiss it on the spot.

### Answering tells you something

**What changed:**
- After you answer, the card says what you did, when it comes back, and — the first time it happens — that a verse or a note is holding now.
- At the end of a sitting: what you returned to, and how much of it is holding. Never a count of what is left.
- The button that opens what you were asked about now says where it takes you: check my note, check the verse, open the Thread.

**How it helps you:**
- You can tell that your answer was recorded, and roughly where you stand, without opening anything.

### More kinds of question

**What changed:**
- New questions about your notes, your links and your Threads: what in the text led you to write this, what has become clearer since, where do these two pull against each other, and what one sentence sits at the centre of this Thread.
- Two new steps at the top of the verse ladder. Once you can write a verse from memory, Harvous will shuffle its phrases and ask you to put them back in order, and later show you a fragment and ask which passage it comes from.
- These two are the only questions Harvous marks, and it marks them against the text itself. Everything else is still an open question that only you can answer.
- Two brand-new questions about two different notes no longer come out identically worded.

**How it helps you:**
- The same note asked six times is six different questions, so you are recalling rather than rereading.
- A verse you already know well has somewhere further to go.

### Your study, on Activity

**What changed:**
- The section on your Activity view is now called Review, not Study Inbox.
- The line about a theme you keep returning to now works however many notes you have. It used to disappear entirely once your library grew past one page.

**How it helps you:**
- An inbox is something other people fill and you fall behind on. This is your own study coming back to you.
