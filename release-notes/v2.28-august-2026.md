# What's New in Harvous v2.28

**Release Date:** August 2026
**Version:** 2.28.0 - 2.28.3

Two things for churches. The Planner got room to work in, and your church now has a shelf.

---

## Updates in This Release

### The Planner, at full size

**What changed:**
- The Planner opens across the whole pane instead of the sidebar column
- Three ways to look at the same plan: a board you drag weeks around on, a calendar, and a list
- The sermon editor is a rail down the side now, not a window over the top

**How it helps you:**
- You can see the quarter you are planning while you edit a week of it
- Dragging a sermon to another Sunday is one motion, rather than opening it, changing the date and saving
- An idea with no date yet can sit in the plan waiting for one, instead of living in your head

### A shelf for your church

**What changed:**
- Your church can keep the links and files it teaches from in one place
- Staff choose who each resource is for: everyone in the church, or leaders only
- A resource can belong to the whole church, or to particular rooms, like Youth or the Tuesday study
- Anything on the shelf can be attached to a planned sermon or gathering

**How it helps you:**
- The commentary your pastor keeps recommending is somewhere you can actually find it
- Leaders' notes and answer keys stay with leaders, without a second place to keep them
- When you open this Sunday's sermon, what it draws on is already sitting there
- If your church's plan lapses, the shelf stays readable. You do not lose what you have been given

### A suggestion box

**What changed:**
- Anyone connected to your church can suggest a resource for the shelf
- A pastor or admin reviews suggestions and decides what gets added
- Suggestions are the one thing your church sees your name on

**How it helps you:**
- The talk that helped you can reach the rest of your church
- Your church's shelf is shaped by the people using it, not only by staff
- Everything else stays private, as it was. Harvous does not tell your church what you read, what you wrote, or whether you opened anything at all. A suggestion is different because you chose to send it, and a pastor cannot reply to it without knowing who it came from

### Channels plan what they publish

**What changed:**
- A ministry channel plans content now, rather than meetings
- Its Planner asks for studies and devotionals instead of a gathering time

**How it helps you:**
- A channel that publishes weekly is not pretending to be a room that meets
- The words on screen match what you are actually doing

### Polish

**What changed:**
- Every pane under My Church shows a loading indicator while it is fetching
- The teaching plan opens noticeably faster
- Empty panes now say what belongs there, rather than showing a bare line of text
- Dates in the plan sit in the same place in every view

**How it helps you:**
- You can tell the difference between "still loading" and "there is nothing here"
- Opening the Planner no longer feels like waiting
- A pane you have not filled yet tells you what it is for
