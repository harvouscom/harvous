# What's New in Harvous — November 12, 2026

**Release Date:** November 12, 2026
**Versions:** v3.32.8

---

## Updates in This Release

### Today's passage arrives on time

**What changed:**
- Today's passage now changes over at your midnight, including when Harvous was left open overnight. You no longer need to reload to see the new day's reading.
- The day's reading is now ready before the day starts, wherever you are.

**How it helps you:**
- The passage you open in the morning is always today's.
