# What's New in Harvous — August 13, 2026

**Release Date:** August 13, 2026
**Versions:** v2.54.8–v2.55.2

A round of fixes for things people ran into in ordinary use — adding a note, selecting folders, quoting a passage, and a few places the app was slower or noisier than it should have been. Later in the day, a shared space learned to say when and where it meets, and Threads got easier to rearrange.

---

## Updates in This Release

### Adding a note happens the moment you tap

**What changed:**

- Adding a note from today's passage, from a sermon starter, or from a suggestion on Home used to disable the button and wait for the server before anything appeared
- Now the editor opens straight away, with the passage or the sermon title already in it, and saving happens quietly behind you

**How it helps you:**

- You can start writing immediately instead of waiting to find out whether the tap worked
- The difference is most noticeable on a phone or a slow connection, which is exactly where the wait used to be longest

### Home stops moving while it loads

**What changed:**

- Home used to appear and then rearrange itself as the last few pieces arrived — cards sliding down, suggestions swapping places under your finger
- It now waits until everything is ready and appears once, settled
- A suggestion could also show up twice in the same set. It can't now

**How it helps you:**

- What you reach for stays where it was when you decided to reach for it

### Settings open on the first tap

**What changed:**

- On a phone, and especially in the installed app, opening a section of Settings could show an empty panel for a moment, so the tap felt ignored
- Settings now shows you it is loading, and starts fetching the moment your finger goes down rather than when you lift it
- A row in a list could also stay highlighted after you closed the note you opened from it, as though it were still selected

**How it helps you:**

- Taps feel answered rather than swallowed
- Lists stop looking like something is selected when nothing is

### Selecting works outside the notes list again

**What changed:**

- Choosing "Select" in folders, Threads, highlights, resources, or search results left you with no way to actually select anything — the checkboxes only appeared once something was already selected
- Selecting now works in every list that offers it
- Scripture no longer offers a Select option, because it never had anything to select

**How it helps you:**

- You can pin or delete several folders or Threads at once again, the same way you can with notes

### Quoting a passage no longer leaves a gap

**What changed:**

- Inserting a quote from a passage added a blank line after it, which landed in the middle of your writing if you were quoting partway through a note
- A quote inserted at the end of a note still leaves you a line to keep typing on

**How it helps you:**

- A quote drops in where you put it without pushing your own words apart

### Content that duplicated across devices

**What changed:**

- In some cases a note edited in the installed app and then opened on a desktop could show part of its content twice — and deleting the duplicate brought it back the next time the other device opened the note
- The cause was a mismatch between what your device had saved and what the server had rewritten, which made the app think there was unsaved work to add back. That is fixed

**How it helps you:**

- A note holds what you wrote, and deleting something means it stays deleted

### A passage suggestion opens the passage

**What changed:**

- Tapping "a passage you keep returning to" on Home showed you a list of your notes on it rather than the passage itself

**How it helps you:**

- The suggestion now does what it says, and you can read the text you keep coming back to

### A study note stops calling itself sermon notes

**What changed:**

- A note that began as sermon notes kept the sermon heading above it everywhere it went, including inside a shared space study that had nothing to do with that sermon
- The heading now only appears where the sermon is actually what you are working on

**How it helps you:**

- A note in a study reads as part of that study

### The Planner shows which room you are planning

**What changed:**

- In a space paired with a channel, the Planner's toggle for switching between the two rendered as an empty grey pill — the room names were there but had no width to be drawn in
- Both names now show, and the toggle works the way the church version already did
- The Ideas column no longer says "No date yet" underneath its name, which only repeated what the column is for

**How it helps you:**

- You can see which of the two rooms you are planning, and switch between them

### A shared space can say when and where it meets

**What changed:**

- A shared space can now carry a meeting day and time — set it when you create the space, or later under Manage space, where Meeting has its own page
- It can also say whether you meet in person, online, or both, and an online room can hold the link you meet on
- The day and place show on the space's About card, and the Planner now starts its weeks on the day the room actually gathers rather than always on Sunday
- Nothing here schedules or sends anything. It is a label on the room and a starting point for the Planner

**How it helps you:**

- A room that meets on Wednesdays reads like one, to everyone who joins it
- Whoever plans the gatherings starts on the right day instead of counting across from Sunday
- The meeting link lives with the room, so nobody has to dig for it in a message thread

**One thing worth knowing:** the meeting link is only ever shown to people who have already joined. It is deliberately left off invite links and the public join page, because an invite goes to people who are not in the room yet.

### Rearranging a Thread works on a phone

**What changed:**

- Reordering the notes in a Thread only worked with a mouse. On a phone or tablet there was no way to do it at all
- Press and hold a note for a moment and you can now drag it into place, on any device
- Reordering a Thread also no longer turns it into a study plan — the order sticks on its own, without adding step numbers or a current step you did not ask for

**How it helps you:**

- You can put a study in the order you want to read it from wherever you happen to be
- Choosing an order and running a study plan are separate decisions again

### Threads look like the rest of the app

**What changed:**

- The trail of connected notes now sits in the same grouped card used elsewhere in the app, whether you open a Thread from the sidebar, from a note, or inside a shared space
- The line joining the notes is still there, and the note you are on is marked by a filled check rather than a highlighted row and a label saying the same thing twice
- Thread lists no longer flash "no Threads" for a moment before the loading indicator appears

**How it helps you:**

- A Thread looks the same wherever you opened it from
- You stop seeing an empty list that was never empty

### Smaller things

**What changed:**

- Adding a resource now grows the button into the form instead of swapping one for the other
- A list's reorder handle no longer stays lit after you finish dragging, in Threads and in the space switcher, where the space's own icon stayed hidden behind it until you clicked elsewhere

**How it helps you:**

- Opening the form is easier to follow, and lists stop looking like something is still being dragged
