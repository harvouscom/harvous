# What's New in Harvous v2.19.0

**Release Date:** August 4, 2026
**Version:** 2.19.0

This release gives churches a way to plan what they are teaching, and gives the people who attend them a one-tap way into notes on it.

---

## Updates in This Release

### This Sunday

If you have picked your church in Settings, Home now shows what your church is teaching next, with a single tap into your own notes on it.

**What changed:**

- Home shows the next service your church has planned: what it is called, and the series it belongs to
- "Start notes" makes a new note in My Home with the passage already set up as a scripture pill and your church's own note template already in the body
- If you have already started notes for that service, the card opens them instead of making a second one
- The card stays for a few days after the service, so writing up Sunday on a Monday still works
- It appears only if you are connected to a church that has planned something, and it disappears again when there is nothing to show

**How it helps you:**

- You arrive on Sunday already knowing the passage, and leave with notes in your own words rather than a photo of a slide
- Your notes are anchored to the passage, so they come back to you later through Review like any other note you write
- What you write stays yours — your church can see that it published a service, never who took notes on it or what they wrote

### Teaching plan, for church staff

Churches on a Harvous plan can now plan their teaching ahead, and every service they add becomes a "This Sunday" card for the people connected to them.

**What changed:**

- A new Teaching plan section in My Church, where staff add a service with a date, a title, a passage, and the series it belongs to
- The passage does not clutter the card people see — it is what their notes open with
- Typing a series name suggests the ones your church has already used, so weeks group together without retyping
- Each service can point at one of your church's note templates, which is what people's notes start from
- Passages are checked as you save, so a mistyped reference is caught before it reaches anyone's notes rather than turning into a dead link in them
- Only one service per date, so there is never a question about which one is "this Sunday"
- Available to staff with a pastor, teacher, or admin role — not to everyone on the team

**How it helps you:**

- The plan you already keep somewhere else now does something: it puts your passage in front of your congregation before Sunday
- Removing or changing a service never touches notes people have already written

### Church plan pricing

**What changed:**

- The Harvous plan for churches is now 30 dollars a month, or 216 dollars a year
- Paying yearly saves 40 percent

**How it helps you:**

- The yearly price is the one most churches will want, and it is now a large enough difference to be worth choosing deliberately

## Notes

- Nothing about church features appears for anyone who has not connected a church in Settings
- If a church's plan lapses, its staff cannot add to the plan, but everything already planned stays visible and everyone keeps their notes
