# What's New in Harvous — August 23, 2026

**Release Date:** August 23, 2026
**Version:** v2.84.6

---

## Updates in This Release

### Editing on your phone

**What changed:**
- Typing on a phone no longer trips a "Too many changes at once" message during normal use.
- The verse-range picker no longer collapses to a couple of rows when your keyboard is open —
  it now uses the full space above it.
- Tapping a suggested Bible reference in a note is more reliable.
- The selection menu that appears when you highlight text no longer floats awkwardly over an
  open passage card.
- The formatting toolbar no longer shows a button as active when it isn't, or flickers while
  you type.

**How it helps you:**
- Editing a note on your phone should now feel as steady as it does on a computer.
