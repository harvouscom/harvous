# What's New in Harvous - October 9, 2026

**Release Date:** October 9, 2026
**Version:** v3.19.1

The words in your Home greeting now take you to what they name, and leave suggestions to the Suggestions section.

---

## Updates in This Release

### Home greeting

**What changed:**
- Tapping the theme in "lately returning to..." now searches your notes for it, instead of offering to make a Thread.
- When the greeting says two passages keep surfacing together, tapping either one opens that passage in the reader.

**How it helps you:**
- The greeting no longer asks the same question as the Suggestions section right below it. Offers to gather notes into a Thread stay in one place.
- The search icon on the chip now does what it shows.
