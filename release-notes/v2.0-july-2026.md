# What's New in Harvous v2.0.1

**Release Date:** July 7, 2026
**Version:** 2.0.1

Shared Spaces foundation work ships on the 2.0 release line, including hosting limits, invite management, and polish across the shared-space dashboard.

---

## Shared Spaces

- Add the Shared Spaces upgrade page with clear limits: up to 10 spaces you can host and up to 30 people per space.
- Manage people and invite links from the space sheet, including link expiration presets.
- See remaining capacity when you own the add-on or host a space.

## Study experience

- New notes in a shared space stay in that space when you compose from the dashboard.
- The note-details panel button appears only after a note is saved, matching the other note toolbar actions.

---

## Tips for Using New Features

- Open **Settings > Add-ons** or visit `/addon` to add Shared Spaces.
- From a space you host, use the gear icon to manage people, invite links, and space settings.
- Contact support from the People sheet when a space reaches its member limit.
