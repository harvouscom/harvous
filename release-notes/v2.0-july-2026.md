# What's New in Harvous v2.0.7

**Release Date:** July 8, 2026
**Version:** 2.0.7

This release includes improvements to navigation, organization, and overall user experience.

---

## Updates in This Release

### Study together in Shared Spaces

**What changed:**
- Highlight text on another member's note to leave a shared annotation (their note stays read-only)
- Space owners can start a group study thread and pin it on the shared dashboard
- Members can attach new notes to an existing group study when composing
- Dashboard shows who added notes since your last visit; the space switcher shows a subtle unseen dot

**How it helps you:**
- Keep conversation on the passage instead of copying notes around
- Run a semester study with one pinned thread everyone adds to
- See at a glance what changed in a space while you were away

### Shared Spaces hosting and billing

**What changed:**
- Clerk billing webhooks now persist the Shared Spaces add-on entitlement automatically after checkout

**How it helps you:**
- Your add-on activates reliably without manual support steps after you subscribe
