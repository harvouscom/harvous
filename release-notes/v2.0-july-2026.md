# What's New in Harvous v2.0.14

**Release Date:** July 10, 2026
**Version:** 2.0.14

This July update introduces a clearer way to study with a group while keeping ownership of the notes you write.
Shared Spaces will become available as final launch verification completes.

---

## Updates in This Release

### Your notes stay in My Home

**What changed:**
- Every note you write remains in My Home, even when you share it with one or more spaces.
- Use **Add to space** to share your own note without creating another version.
- Use **Save a copy** when you want an independent, attributed copy of someone else's note.

**How it helps you:**
- You can study with different groups without losing track of your original notes.
- Changes to your note stay consistent wherever you have added it, while each space keeps its own organization.

### Conversations and Threads in context

**What changed:**
- Activity shows responses to a note and groups them by space when you are viewing the note from My Home.
- A space owner chooses the current Thread.
- Members can follow that Thread, attach their own notes that are already in the space, and respond to passages.

**How it helps you:**
- You can return to a group conversation without placing another person's comments directly into your private
  note view.
- Everyone can contribute while each author remains in control of their own writing.

### Recovery and privacy

**What changed:**
- Owners can restore a deleted shared space for 30 days.
- Removing a note from a space does not delete the original note from My Home.
- Locked notes stay out of shared spaces, and invite previews do not reveal note content.
- Leaving or being removed from a space removes access while preserving each person's own notes.

**How it helps you:**
- Accidental space deletion has a recovery window.
- Your private and locked content stays outside the group, and your authored notes remain yours.

---

## Tips for Using New Features

- **Share an existing note:** Open a note you wrote and choose **Add to space**.
- **Keep a personal copy:** Choose **Save a copy** on another person's note.
- **Review responses:** Open Activity from a note to see where people have responded.
- **Follow the study:** Open the current Thread in a shared space and attach one of your own notes.
