# What's New in Harvous — August 4, 2026

**Release Date:** August 4, 2026
**Versions:** v2.14.0–v2.19.0

Harvous comes to churches. Churches can publish study to the people who attend them and plan what they are teaching ahead; those people can follow along, and start their own notes on Sunday's passage with one tap.

---

## Updates in This Release

### Harvous for churches

Churches can now publish study to the people who attend them, and those people can follow along in their own Harvous.

**What changed:**

- Churches publish study through ministry channels — one channel per ministry or study context, such as adult education, students, or a sermon series companion
- Once you pick your church in Settings, you can browse the channels it publishes and follow the ones you want
- Study from the channels you follow appears on Home under "From your church"
- Anything your church publishes stays read-only, and you can start your own note from it whenever something is worth keeping in your own words
- Church staff can invite and manage their own team, and set up channels themselves
- Churches can start on a pilot and subscribe when they are ready

**How it helps you:**

- Your church's teaching and your own notes live in the same place
- You choose what you follow, and you can stop following at any time
- What you write stays yours — following a church never gives anyone access to your notes

### This Sunday

If you have picked your church in Settings, Home now shows what your church is teaching next, with a single tap into your own notes on it.

**What changed:**

- Home shows the next service your church has planned: what it is called, and the series it belongs to
- "Start notes" makes a new note in My Home with the passage already set up as a scripture pill and your church's own note template already in the body
- If you have already started notes for that service, the card opens them instead of making a second one
- The card stays for a few days after the service, so writing up Sunday on a Monday still works
- It appears only if you are connected to a church that has planned something, and it disappears again when there is nothing to show

**How it helps you:**

- You arrive on Sunday already knowing the passage, and leave with notes in your own words rather than a photo of a slide
- Your notes are anchored to the passage, so they come back to you later through Review like any other note you write
- What you write stays yours — your church can see that it published a service, never who took notes on it or what they wrote

### Teaching plan, for church staff

Churches on a Harvous plan can now plan their teaching ahead, and every service they add becomes a "This Sunday" card for the people connected to them.

**What changed:**

- A new Teaching plan section in My Church, where staff add a service with a date, a title, a passage, and the series it belongs to
- The passage does not clutter the card people see — it is what their notes open with
- Typing a series name suggests the ones your church has already used, so weeks group together without retyping
- Each service can point at one of your church's note templates, which is what people's notes start from
- Passages are checked as you save, so a mistyped reference is caught before it reaches anyone's notes rather than turning into a dead link in them
- Only one service per date, so there is never a question about which one is "this Sunday"
- Available to staff with a pastor, teacher, or admin role — not to everyone on the team

**How it helps you:**

- The plan you already keep somewhere else now does something: it puts your passage in front of your congregation before Sunday
- Removing or changing a service never touches notes people have already written

### A quicker sidebar

**What changed:**

- The space menu now lists everything in one place: My Home, your church, and the spaces inside each of them
- Any space is one click away, including a church channel while you are looking at My Home
- Everything in the menu lines up in a single column, so it reads as one list instead of a staircase
- Switching between the space view and the list view is still a single click on either button
- A long church name now stays on one line and slides to reveal itself when you hover

**How it helps you:**

- Fewer clicks to get where you are going
- The menu is easier to scan at a glance
- Nothing shifts around as you move between spaces

### Your writing survives more

**What changed:**

- A note you were part way through writing stays open across a refresh, without a toast interrupting you about it
- Two saves of the same note can no longer overtake each other and land as a conflict with yourself
- When a real conflict happens, it says who else saved, and resolving it no longer discards the newer version

**How it helps you:**

- Closing a tab by accident does not cost you a paragraph
- "Someone else edited this" now means someone else actually did

### Church plan pricing

**What changed:**

- The Harvous plan for churches is now 30 dollars a month, or 216 dollars a year
- Paying yearly saves 40 percent

**How it helps you:**

- The yearly price is the one most churches will want, and it is now a large enough difference to be worth choosing deliberately

---

## Tips for Using New Features

- **Set your church:** Go to Settings, then My Church, and pick your church from the directory
- **Follow what you want:** Open My Church to see the channels your church publishes, and follow the ones that fit
- **Make it yours:** On a note from your church, use "Save a copy" to start your own version

---

## Notes

- Nothing about church features appears for anyone who has not connected a church in Settings
- If a church's plan lapses, its staff cannot add to the plan, but everything already planned stays visible and everyone keeps their notes
