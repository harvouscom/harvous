# What's New in Harvous — September 18, 2026

**Release Date:** September 18, 2026
**Version:** v3.12.0

Churches get the pieces that were missing between a pastor planning a series and a congregation walking it, and everyone gets every note they have written on a passage in one place.

---

## Updates in This Release

### Your notes on this passage

**What changed:**
- When you open a verse, the passage card now lists every note you have written on it, newest first
- A note counts when its passage covers the verse, including longer passages that run across chapters
- The looser connections, such as notes from the same section or on a shared theme, now sit underneath as Connected notes

**How it helps you:**
- The second time through a passage, what you wrote the first time is right there
- You see the notes that really are about this verse, not just nearby ones

### Study plans that travel

**What changed:**
- A sermon series on your church's plan can be published into a channel as a study plan
- Anyone following a channel can take its study plan into their own study, and a group leader can hand it to their group
- A channel's page now shows the study it is walking through
- The study plan page is simpler: your progress sits on one line under the title, and "I finished this study" waits under the last step

**How it helps you:**
- Plan an eight-week series once and your congregation can walk it week by week
- Small groups can follow the church's study without anyone retyping it

### For church staff

**What changed:**
- You can start a note right from a channel you run. It stays private until you choose to publish it there
- A new "Get your church set up" card walks a new pastor through the first steps in order
- You can import a folder of files straight into a channel or group
- Invite links for your church's groups no longer need a personal subscription
- Only church admins can see or change the church's plan

**How it helps you:**
- Writing for your congregation starts where you already are, with no half-written note showing up early
- A new staff member knows what to do first
- Years of sermon notes can move in all at once

### For your congregation

**What changed:**
- New study from your church is marked New on Home until you open it
- You can turn on a notification when channels you follow share something. It is off unless you choose it, and it comes at most once a day
- If you change or leave your church, you stop following its channels
- If your church is no longer on Harvous, Settings says so instead of going quiet

**How it helps you:**
- You can see at a glance what arrived since you last looked
- Moving churches does not leave you with the old one's study

### Fixes

**What changed:**
- Choosing "Every day" for reminders now saves. It was quietly switching back to Sunday and midweek
- Opening a series in the Planner no longer shows an error page

**How it helps you:**
- Reminders arrive on the schedule you picked
