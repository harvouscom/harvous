# What's New in Harvous v2.10.0

**Release Date:** July 31, 2026
**Version:** 2.10.0

You can now study a note together. In a shared space, the person who wrote a note can open it up so other members can edit it too, one person at a time, so nobody writes over anybody.

---

## Updates in This Release

### Editing a note together

**What changed:**
- Open any note you wrote in a shared space using "Let others edit" in the note details panel.
- When it is open, a member can tap "Start editing" and write in it directly.
- Only one person edits at a time. Everyone else sees the note update as it is written, and the banner at the top says who is writing.
- The pen passes on its own when the person writing closes the note or steps away, so it is never stuck with someone who has walked off.
- The note stays yours. Your name still leads the byline, other people are credited as editors, and only you can delete it or take it out of a space.

**How it helps you:**
- A group can build one set of notes on a passage instead of five separate ones.
- Taking turns means you never lose a sentence to someone typing at the same moment.
- You decide, note by note, what the group can work on.

### What stays private

**What changed:**
- Editing is off by default on every note, including notes already sitting in a shared space.
- Locked notes can never be opened for editing. They stay yours alone.
- Turning a note's editing back off, or removing it from a space, ends other people's access straight away.

**How it helps you:**
- Sharing a note to a space still means sharing it to read, exactly as before. Nothing you have already shared has changed.

### Watching someone write

**What changed:**
- When a note is open for co-editing, people watching see the text update as it is saved — usually under a second — without refreshing the page.

**How it helps you:**
- You can follow along while someone else has the pen, instead of waiting for a full reload.

### Mac and iPhone

**What changed:**
- Notes open for co-editing stay read-only on Mac and iPhone. Editing still happens on the web, where the pen is tracked.
- Saving a note from Mac or iPhone no longer quietly overwrites a newer copy from another device.

**How it helps you:**
- You can keep a co-edited note open on your phone to follow along without risking an accidental overwrite.
- Edits made on the web and on your devices stay in sync more reliably.

### Shared space tidying

**What changed:**
- If a space has no current Thread yet, members no longer see an empty "Current Thread" heading explaining that there isn't one.

**How it helps you:**
- Less to scroll past on the way to the notes.

---

## Tips for Using New Features

- **Find the switch:** Open a note in a shared space, tap the details button, and look for "Let others edit" under Editing.
- **Watch the banner:** The line at the top of a shared note always tells you whether you can write in it right now, and who has it if you cannot.
- **Take your turn:** When it says "Open for editing," tap "Start editing." Tap "Done" when you want to hand it back.
- **Follow along on native:** Open the same note on Mac or iPhone to read while someone else writes on the web.
