# What's New in Harvous — August 8, 2026

**Release Date:** August 8, 2026
**Versions:** v2.37.4–v2.43.0

A Thread can now be a study plan a group walks through together, week by week — and a church can hand one to a room straight from its sermon plan.

---

## Updates in This Release

### Study plans

**What changed:**
- A Thread can become a study plan: an order you choose, and a step the group is on
- The person leading sets the current step, and can move steps earlier or later
- Steps past the current one are dimmed, so it reads as "not yet" rather than a wall
- The space's Current Thread card shows how far along you are — "Step 3 of 8"

**How it helps you:**
- Your group knows where it is without anyone posting "we're on week four"
- You can read ahead if you want to; dimming sets the pace, it does not lock a door
- Reordering the weeks does not move the group — they stay on the step they were on

### Publish a series as a study plan

**What changed:**
- A sermon series in a ministry's plan can be published into that room as a study plan
- Each week becomes a step, in the order you planned it
- Adding week nine and publishing again appends just that week

**How it helps you:**
- The study your church already planned reaches the room without retyping it
- Republishing never duplicates the weeks already there, and never restarts the group
- Publishing does not take a room off a study it is already in the middle of

### Your ministry's next gathering

**What changed:**
- A ministry's own next gathering now shows to the people in that room, not only to staff
- One tap starts a note on it, the same way the church's Sunday service already worked
- The card turns into "Open my note" once you have written something

**How it helps you:**
- Youth, small groups, and classes get the same one-tap start Sunday has had
- You arrive with the passage already in front of you

### Sharing a plan

**What changed:**
- A published study plan can be shared as a public preview
- The preview shows each week's title and passage, and nothing else
- When the room has an open invite, the preview offers a way to join

**How it helps you:**
- You can show someone what a study covers before asking them to join
- Nothing anyone wrote is on that page — the preview lists the plan, not the notes

### For church staff

**What changed:**
- A Scripture map showing which books your church has taught from, and how often
- Leaders can see how many people have opened each step of a study plan
- Every room now has a Library shelf of what your church has set aside for it

**How it helps you:**
- You can see at a glance whether a year has been spent in four books or forty
- You know whether the group is walking with you before you plan week five
- The map counts what you planned; it never reads anyone's notes
- Step counts are numbers only. Never who, and never shown to the room

### Fixes and polish

**What changed:**
- The sidebar no longer shows the page behind it when it floats over your notes on a phone
- "New space" and "New channel" keep their names on a narrow screen instead of becoming a bare plus
- You can start a series straight from the plan, without opening the full planner
- Fixed a crash, a phantom collaborator, and a retry that could not succeed
- Select several notes and act on them at once
- A re-run that stops short now says so, instead of showing an empty run
- Renaming a series judges the name and its run together, so re-runs stay distinct
- The note picker's height cap is on the right element again

**How it helps you:**
- Fewer dead ends, and clearer answers when something does not go through

---

## Tips for Using the New Features

### Study plans:
- **Quick tip:** Open a Thread's menu and choose "Make it a study plan" to turn it into one.
- **Pro tip:** Turning it back into a collection keeps the notes and the order — nothing is lost.

### Publishing a series:
- **Quick tip:** Open a series in the Planner and choose "Publish as a study plan".
- **Pro tip:** Come back and publish again after adding weeks; only the new ones are added.
