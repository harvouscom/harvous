## Updates in This Release

### Put a note on any plan you keep

**What changed:**
- A note's details panel now asks *which* plan. Sermons, or any ministry room you run — youth, kids, a class, a small group — each listed with the date picker following your choice.
- It used to offer the church's sermon plan and nothing else, so a note could only ever become a sermon even though the planner itself has always let you switch between these plans.
- If you run a room but do not set the church's Sunday, you now have somewhere to put a note where before you had nothing.

**How it helps you:**
- The note you wrote on Tuesday can become next week's youth study, not only a sermon.

### Fixes

**What changed:**
- Removing something from a plan no longer leaves the notes written for it claiming to be scheduled for a week that no longer exists.
- A note's details panel now knows when it is already on a plan, and says so instead of offering to add it a second time.

### A church can hand its congregation a study

**What changed:**
- A ministry channel can now carry a study plan. Publish a sermon series as a plan and the people who follow that channel walk it week by week, with the steps in the order you set.
- Until now a study plan could only live in a small group's space — the one room that reaches a whole congregation was the one room that could not hold it.
- Following a plan asks nothing of the reader: staff write the steps, everyone else reads them in order and takes their own notes. Followers still cannot post into a channel.

**How it helps you:**
- The study you plan on Sunday can reach the people who heard it, without being retyped into somewhere else.
