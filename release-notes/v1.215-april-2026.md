# What's New in Harvous v1.215

**Release Date:** April 13–15, 2026  
**Version:** 1.215.86 – 1.215.93 (see `package.json` for the current app version)

This stretch of releases focuses on **navigation you can trust**, **cleaner thread removal**, **snappier loading** for your profile and spaces, **polish** across search, notes, and mobile panels, and a clearer **Share Space** flow when you open sharing from a space.

---

## Updates in This Release

### Persistent navigation and Home

**What changed:**

- Dismissed threads in the side nav are less likely to **pop back** on the Home dashboard after you close them. The app only treats a thread as “open again” when you are actually viewing it, not when the app refreshes data in the background.
- Links from Home to a thread or note no longer pick up a **stale space** from saved state, so you land in the context you expect.

**How it helps you:**

- Your nav bar behaves more like intentional history: dismiss stays dismiss until you choose that thread again.
- Fewer “why did that thread come back?” moments on Home.

---

### Thread removal and My Pile

**What changed:**

- **Deleting or erasing a thread** is handled more consistently across the app, including threads in your personal pile and unorganized notes.
- Related lists and flows stay in sync so removed threads do not linger in confusing ways.

**How it helps you:**

- When you remove a thread, the app reflects that decision more reliably so you are not second-guessing whether it really went away.

---

### Faster profile and space data

**What changed:**

- The server and app work together to load **profile** and **space** information with less redundant work behind the scenes.

**How it helps you:**

- Spaces and your account details should feel a bit quicker and more stable when you open them, especially if you switch spaces often.

---

### Search (Spotlight) and discovery

**What changed:**

- **Spotlight search** and result rows got layout and interaction polish on both desktop and mobile.
- **Recent searches** and related UI were tightened so common lookups feel smoother.

**How it helps you:**

- Finding a note or thread by name or keyword is a little faster and clearer, with fewer rough edges when you open search from the dock or keyboard.

---

### Notes, cards, and Scripture compare

**What changed:**

- **Note cards and the editor** received small usability and styling improvements (including how long notes scroll and read on the page).
- **Scripture compare** and surrounding panels (menus, bottom sheets on small screens, desktop panel layout) were adjusted for clearer actions and less clutter.

**How it helps you:**

- Reading and comparing Scripture next to your notes should feel more comfortable, especially on phone and tablet.

---

### Offline and PWA behavior

**What changed:**

- Minor improvements to how the app signals **offline** state and starts up as an **installed app** (PWA), so status is easier to notice without getting in your way.

**How it helps you:**

- You get a clearer sense of when you are on- or offline without noisy UI.

---

### Share Space

**What changed:**

- Opening **Share** for a space uses a dedicated **Share Space** panel (desktop side panel and mobile sheet). Owners can switch the space between **private** and **shared (link)**, copy the share link, and generate a **new link** when you need to rotate it.
- If the space already has **other members**, you get a clear warning before making it private again, since that removes their access.
- If your **plan** limits how many shared spaces you can have, turning a space public may show an **upgrade** path instead of leaving you without an explanation.

**How it helps you:**

- Sharing settings and links for a space live in one obvious place, with fewer surprises when visibility or membership changes.

---

### Reliability and updates

**What changed:**

- Regular **version and cache updates** so your browser picks up the latest scripts and styles after we ship.

**How it helps you:**

- You are more likely to see the newest fixes the first time you open Harvous after an update.

---

## Tips for Using New Features

- **Dismissed nav items:** If a thread reappears after you dismissed it, open that thread once on purpose, then dismiss again; if it still misbehaves, use **Help** or support so we can follow up.
- **Search:** Try your usual keywords; recent searches should speed up repeat lookups.
- **Threads:** After deleting a thread, give the list a moment to refresh; if something looks off, pull to refresh or revisit the space from the switcher.
