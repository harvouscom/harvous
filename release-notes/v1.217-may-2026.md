# What's New in Harvous v1.217.5

**Release Date:** May 30, 2026
**Version:** 1.217.5

This release includes improvements to navigation, organization, and overall user experience.

---

## Updates in This Release

### General improvements

**What changed:**
- New.harvous.com subdomain defaulting to prototype shell
- Unify pill sizing across iOS, Mac, and Prototype
- Ease Aw Snap by skipping IndexedDB bootstrap on /prototype
- Coerce Date values in CSV export escape helper

**How it helps you:**
- Makes your Bible study workflow smoother
- Smoother, more reliable experience

### Organization and structure

**What changed:**
- Sync deletion log, cascade delete, personal home space, import parsing
- Hide space switcher when account has one owned space

**How it helps you:**
- Better organization keeps your notes tidy
- Accurate information you can trust

### Writing and editing

**What changed:**
- Let Shift+letter type in text fields before shortcuts

**How it helps you:**
- Smoother, more reliable experience
