# What's New in Harvous — September 24, 2026

**Release Date:** September 24, 2026
**Version:** v3.12.1

Notifications take you to what they are about, and a few everyday rough edges are smoothed out.

---

## Updates in This Release

### Notifications that go somewhere

**What changed:**
- Tapping a verse reminder opens the Bible at that passage
- Tapping "New from your church" opens the channel with the most new posts

**How it helps you:**
- One tap from the lock screen to the thing the notification was about

### Knowing where something is new

**What changed:**
- The space menu now shows a dot on your church when any of its spaces has something new, and on "See all" when a hidden space does
- My Home shows a dot when today's suggestions are waiting
- The "Whose study" menu on Activity shows the same dots next to each space

**How it helps you:**
- The dot on the toolbar always points you to a place in the menu

### Activity keeps your place

**What changed:**
- Switching whose study you are looking at keeps the day you were on

**How it helps you:**
- Compare the same day across your spaces without paging back each time

### Tidier buttons

**What changed:**
- Save, Cancel and Remove now sit on one row, with Save on the right, and only wrap when there is not room

**How it helps you:**
- Edit forms are shorter, and removing something sits apart from saving it
