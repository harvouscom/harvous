# What's New in Harvous — September 24, 2026

**Release Date:** September 24, 2026
**Versions:** v3.12.1 - v3.13.0

Search now reaches the whole Bible, review cards got roomier, notifications take you to what they are about, and a few everyday rough edges are smoothed out.

---

## Updates in This Release

### Find a verse by its words

**What changed:**
- Type a few words into search and an "In the Bible" section lists the verses that contain them, in your own translation
- The words you typed are shown in bold inside each verse
- Different forms of a word count too: searching "redeem" also finds redeemed and redemption
- Put words in quotes to find them as an exact phrase
- Tap a verse to open it in the Bible, lit up in its chapter
- The Scripture tab shows more verses than the first few under Everything

**How it helps you:**
- The verse you half remember is findable even if you never wrote it down
- "Love your enemies" takes you to Matthew 5 and Luke 6 without knowing where to look

### Review cards with room to think

**What changed:**
- Review exercises now open as a larger card: the question at the top, the verse or note line in the middle, and your answer below
- The first time you fill in the blanks for a passage, the missing words come as tiles to place, mixed with a few that do not belong. After that you type them, as before
- Putting verses in order gives you pieces to arrange
- Questions like "what comes next" show the verse you know, then an empty place where your answer goes

**How it helps you:**
- Less squinting and scrolling while you review
- A gentler first meeting with a passage before you are asked to recall it word for word

### Notifications that go somewhere

**What changed:**
- Tapping a verse reminder opens the Bible at that passage
- Tapping "New from your church" opens the channel with the most new posts

**How it helps you:**
- One tap from the lock screen to the thing the notification was about

### Knowing where something is new

**What changed:**
- The space menu now shows a dot on your church when any of its spaces has something new, and on "See all" when a hidden space does
- My Home shows a dot when today's suggestions are waiting
- The "Whose study" menu on Activity shows the same dots next to each space

**How it helps you:**
- The dot on the toolbar always points you to a place in the menu

### Activity keeps your place

**What changed:**
- Switching whose study you are looking at keeps the day you were on

**How it helps you:**
- Compare the same day across your spaces without paging back each time

### Tidier results and buttons

**What changed:**
- Every search result now leads with a small icon for what it is, lined up the same way as when you browse
- Save, Cancel and Remove now sit on one row, with Save on the right, and only wrap when there is not room
- The "What are you studying today?" prompt on Activity now types itself out

**How it helps you:**
- A long list of results is easier to scan at a glance
- Edit forms are shorter, and removing something sits apart from saving it
