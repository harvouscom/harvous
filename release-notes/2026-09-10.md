# What's New in Harvous — September 10, 2026

**Release Date:** September 2026
**Version:** v3.9.5

Review got a lot better at asking. Questions about your own notes now quote a whole line you
actually wrote, there are new kinds of exercise drawing on your highlights and the places a
passage names, and you can choose which kinds you get.

---

## Updates in This Release

### Better questions about your notes

**What changed:**
- When Review asks which of your notes a line came from, it now quotes a whole sentence you wrote
- Passages you quoted inside a note are never used as the line, and neither are headings, bare references, or rows of scripture pills
- A long sentence is trimmed at a natural pause and marked so you can see it is only part of one
- The line on the Activity row and the line on the card are now always the same

**How it helps you:**
- The line you are shown is a thought you had, not twelve words cut out of the middle of a paragraph
- You are no longer asked to recognise your own note by words you did not write
- If you highlighted something in the note, that is what you are shown, in the list as well as on the card

### New kinds of exercise

**What changed:**
- Pick the words you marked in a verse, among other parts of the same verse
- Pick the verse you marked in a chapter you read
- Pick the place a verse or chapter names
- The free sample now offers four ways to try the same verse: fill in the blanks, first letters, put it in order, or pick what follows

**How it helps you:**
- What you highlighted while reading finally comes back to you as a question
- More variety in what you are asked, so a passage you know well still has something to work on
- If you have not subscribed, you can try more than one kind of exercise before deciding

### A question you get wrong comes back differently

**What changed:**
- Missing a question no longer means meeting exactly the same one next time
- New passages and chapters now open on a range of different questions instead of nearly always the same one

**How it helps you:**
- A second angle on something you did not remember, rather than the same wording again
- The one passage you are struggling with is the one that now varies most
- A queue that had settled into asking one kind of question will start to spread out as new items are added

### See and choose what you are being asked

**What changed:**
- Every question now names its kind, beside the task on a row and in the card's header
- A new Settings page lists the kinds of exercise and lets you turn off the ones you do not want
- How well you hold a passage is now said plainly beside its title, and speaks to you: "You have this", "Give this another look"

**How it helps you:**
- You can see at a glance which questions want typing and which are a tap, and pick the one you have a minute for
- If writing a verse out from memory is not how you want to study, you can say so
- Nothing leaves your queue: the same passages and notes come round, asked about differently

### Fixes

**What changed:**
- The translation picker on the free sample question now works. It was shown, and the verse behind it was always the same translation regardless

**How it helps you:**
- The verse you see is the translation named beside it
