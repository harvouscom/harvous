# What's New in Harvous - October 4, 2026

**Release Date:** October 4, 2026
**Version:** v3.18.5

Notes you start from Scripture now open the way you would write them: the words first, with their source underneath.

---

## Updates in This Release

### Notes from today's passage

**What changed:**
- Tapping Create note on today's passage now opens a note with the passage quoted, and the Scripture pill sitting right under it as its source.
- Untitled notes that open with Scripture already in them now get the date as their title, the same as any other note.
- When you insert a quote under the exact same pill, the pill moves beneath the quote instead of staying above it.

**How it helps you:**
- You start with the passage in front of you instead of a bare pill.
- You no longer end up with a blank title on notes you started from a passage or a verse.
- Quotes read like quotations with a citation, and changing the pill's translation or color still updates the quote above it.
