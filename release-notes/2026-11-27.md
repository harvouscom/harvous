# What's New in Harvous — November 27, 2026

**Release Date:** November 27, 2026
**Versions:** v3.32.10

---

## Updates in This Release

### Today's passage is there when you open Harvous

**What changed:**
- The passage card at the top of Home now shows every time you open the app, not only some of the time.
- If you tapped "Not today" last night, the new day's passage is back in the morning, even if Harvous stayed open overnight.

**How it helps you:**
- The day's reading is waiting for you where the day starts, without a reload.

### What's new cards stay put

**What changed:**
- Cards about what's new in Harvous no longer flash up for a moment and then disappear.
- A card you put away on one device no longer pops up briefly on another.

**How it helps you:**
- What you see when Home opens is what is really there for you.
