# What's New in Harvous — October 31, 2026

**Release Date:** October 31, 2026
**Version:** v3.29.0

The Bible reader can now show the words of Jesus in red, the way many printed Bibles do.

---

## Updates in This Release

### Words of Jesus in red

**What changed:**
- A new "Words of Jesus" setting in the reader's details panel. Set it to Red and His words are coloured throughout the Gospels, Acts and Revelation.
- It works in every translation in the reader, and in both columns when you compare two translations.
- It is off unless you turn it on, and the choice stays with this device.

**How it helps you:**
- You can see at a glance where Jesus is speaking, and where the narration or someone else's words begin.
- If you grew up reading a red-letter Bible, the page looks the way you remember.

**Good to know:**
- Where it is unclear who is speaking in a verse, we leave it black rather than guess.
- Translations differ on a few passages. In John 3:16-21, for example, the NET reads the words as John's commentary rather than Jesus speaking, so they stay black in the NET.
