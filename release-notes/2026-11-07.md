# What's New in Harvous — November 7, 2026

**Release Date:** November 7, 2026
**Version:** v3.32.1 - v3.32.3

Today's passage leads with the words, and two small things that kept getting in your way are fixed.

---

## Updates in This Release

### Today's passage leads with the words

**What changed:**
- Today's passage on Home now opens with the verses themselves, large, under a soft opening quote mark
- The reference sits underneath like a signature, with the translation shown as a small tag beside it, and the two buttons on the same line
- The painted sky behind the passage is gone, and so is the "Today's passage" label above it
- The close button now sits in the top corner of the card

**How it helps you:**
- You read Scripture first, not a title
- The card looks different from the rest of Home, so it is easy to find

### What's new stays put away

**What changed:**
- When you close the What's new card, it now stays closed for all of Harvous 3
- Before, it came back with each new feature release, even if you had already dismissed it

**How it helps you:**
- You see the card once, not again every few days

### Search keeps you in the Thread you opened

**What changed:**
- When you search and open a Thread from the results, the Thread's notes now appear
- Before, the old results stayed on screen under the Thread's name
- Going back returns you to the same results, and typing again takes you back to results

**How it helps you:**
- Opening something from search shows you that thing
