# What's New in Harvous — August 21, 2026

**Release Date:** August 21, 2026
**Versions:** v2.72.17–v2.77.0

The Bible reader can now hold more than one thing open at a time, and it remembers where you actually stopped reading rather than which chapter you were in. Alongside that, a run of fixes to suggestions, links, and the small pieces of text that were moving too fast to read.

---

## Updates in This Release

### The reader holds more than one thing at once

**What changed:**

- Opening a cross-reference no longer closes the passage you opened it from — cards stack beside each other, and the ones you are not reading collapse into chips
- You can reorder the cards, and dismiss any one without losing the others
- The chapter dims around whichever card you bring forward, so the passage you are looking at is the one lit up
- A card pointing somewhere else entirely leaves the chapter alone

**How it helps you:**

- Following a cross-reference is no longer a trade — you keep the verse you started from and the verse it sent you to, side by side
- The chapter behind the cards becomes a way of moving around rather than something you lose track of

### Reading picks up where you stopped

**What changed:**

- Harvous now records the verse you reached, not just the chapter
- "Where you left off reading" returns you to that verse, and says so — "Back to Romans 5:8" rather than "Back to Romans 5"

**How it helps you:**

- Coming back to a long chapter no longer means scrolling to find your place

### Chapters you have already read open instantly

**What changed:**

- A chapter stored on your device now opens from there rather than waiting on the network
- "Loading…" no longer appears for text that was already on the device

**How it helps you:**

- Reading a book you have been through is immediate, and stays immediate on a bad connection or none at all

### Suggestions say what they actually mean

**What changed:**

- A suggestion to connect two notes now names what they share — "Both cite Genesis 1" instead of "Both cite the same passage" — and proposes "Genesis 1" as the thread name rather than joining the two note titles together
- Opening a cross-reference suggestion marks the passage it meant, so you can see which of the related passages it was pointing at
- Cross-references have their own icon instead of borrowing the one threads use
- Suggestions you have finished with stop coming back — creating a thread a suggestion asked for rests it properly
- A dot appears on the Home button when you have not looked at today's suggestions

**How it helps you:**

- A suggestion tells you why it is worth your attention before you tap it
- The shelf stops offering you things you have already done

### Links

**What changed:**

- The editor toolbar has a link button, and Cmd-K works over selected text
- A pasted link is looked up while you are still typing, so its preview is ready by the time you hover it
- Link previews lead with the site name — "The Gospel Coalition" rather than a URL to decipher
- Previews are remembered, so revisiting a note no longer re-fetches every link in it

**How it helps you:**

- Adding a link is where you would expect it to be, rather than something you had to paste
- Hovering a link tells you where it goes without a wait

### Fixes

**What changed:**

- Highlighting a verse no longer underlines its verse number
- Saved references show the colour they were saved in instead of always amber
- Switching spaces with a note open no longer appears to clear the note's folders
- Long titles and captions scroll slowly enough to read, start moving as soon as you point at them, and fade at the edges instead of being cut mid-letter
- Short labels that fit are left alone entirely
- Dragging a study card to reorder it now shows the card, not the empty space beside it

**How it helps you:**

- The reader shows what you marked, in the colour you marked it
- Text that is too long to fit can actually be read rather than glimpsed
