# What's New in Harvous v1.13

**Release Date:** January 2026  
**Version:** 1.13.0 - 1.18.1

This release focuses on improving navigation, organization, and overall user experience across mobile and desktop.

---

## Updates in This Release

### 🎯 Smarter Mobile Navigation

**What changed:**
- The navigation button at the top of your screen now has two distinct areas that do different things
- The button behaves intelligently based on where you are in the app

**How it helps you:**
- **Reading a note?** Tap the main part of the button to go back to your thread. Tap the icon on the right to see all your threads and spaces.
- **Browsing a thread or space?** The main button opens your navigation menu. The icon on the right also opens it - whichever is easier to reach!
- **Already on the page shown?** If the button shows where you currently are (like "My Home" when you're on the home page), tapping the main area opens the navigation menu instead of trying to navigate to where you already are.
- **Inside the navigation menu:** The top button works the same way - tap the main area to go to that space/home, or tap the icon to see your space picker. If you're already on that page, tapping the main area opens the space picker.

**In simple terms:**
Think of it like a smart button that knows what you need:
- Main button area = "Take me there" (or "show me options" if I'm already there)
- Icon on the right = "Show me all my options"

### 🗂️ Better Space & Thread Organization

**What changed:**
- Spaces and threads now remember where you last used them
- The app keeps track of which space you're working in, even as you navigate around

**How it helps you:**
- When you switch between threads, the app remembers which space you were viewing them from
- Your navigation list stays organized the way you left it
- Less confusion about "where am I?" when moving between different parts of your notes

### 📱 Improved Thread Switching

**What changed:**
- Added a helpful prompt when viewing a thread that belongs to a different space than the one you're browsing

**How it helps you:**
- If you're browsing "Bible Study" space but open a thread from "Sermon Notes," the app asks if you want to:
  - Add that thread to your current space, OR
  - Switch to viewing the space where that thread lives
- Makes it easier to keep your spaces organized without getting lost

### ✨ Desktop Navigation Polish

**What changed:**
- Desktop now has a dropdown menu for switching between spaces
- You can "close" spaces from your navigation without deleting them
- Cleaner visual design with better button states

**How it helps you:**
- Quickly jump between your different spaces without leaving the page
- Temporarily hide spaces you're not using right now (they're still there, just hidden from view)
- Easier to see which space or thread you're currently viewing

### 📋 Smarter Copy & Paste

**What changed:**
- When you paste Bible verses (like "John 3:16"), they automatically become clickable verse pills
- Pasted headings are automatically adjusted to fit Harvous's style (H1 becomes H2, etc.)

**How it helps you:**
- No more manual formatting when copying verses from other apps
- Your notes stay consistently styled even when pasting from different sources
- Verse references are instantly clickable to see the full text

### 🔄 Better Sync & Performance

**What changed:**
- Improved how the app syncs your data across devices
- Fixed issues where note counts could temporarily show incorrect numbers
- Better handling of offline mode

**How it helps you:**
- Your notes and threads stay in sync more reliably
- Counts (like "12 notes in this thread") are more accurate
- The app works better when you have a spotty internet connection

## Understanding Harvous's Hierarchy

To help you understand these updates, here's how Harvous organizes your content:

```
My Home (Dashboard)
├── Space 1 (e.g., "Bible Study")
│   ├── Thread 1 (e.g., "Gospel of John")
│   │   ├── Note 1
│   │   ├── Note 2
│   │   └── Note 3
│   └── Thread 2 (e.g., "Prayer Requests")
│       └── Notes...
└── Space 2 (e.g., "Sermon Notes")
    └── Threads...
```

**Spaces** = Big categories (like folders)
**Threads** = Topics within those categories (like notebooks)
**Notes** = Individual entries (like pages)

## Tips for Using the New Features

### Mobile Navigation:
- **Quick tip:** When reading notes, tap the main button area to go back to your thread, or tap the icon to see all options
- **Quick tip:** The icon on the right is always there as a reliable way to open navigation, no matter where you are
- **Pro tip:** The navigation menu remembers your last position, so you can quickly switch between threads you're working on
- **Pro tip:** When you're already on a page (like Home), the button smartly switches to showing you options instead of trying to navigate to where you already are

### Space Management:
- **Quick tip:** Use the dropdown (icon with three lines) to switch spaces without losing your place
- **Pro tip:** "Close" spaces you're not actively using to keep your navigation clean - they're not deleted, just hidden

### Verse Pasting:
- **Quick tip:** Just copy and paste verses from Bible apps - Harvous will automatically format them
- **Pro tip:** Click on any verse pill to see the full text without leaving your note
