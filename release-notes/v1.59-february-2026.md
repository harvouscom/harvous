# What's New in Harvous v1.59.0

**Release Date:** February 1, 2026
**Version:** 1.59.0

This release adds **Lock notes with PIN**—encrypt any note with a 4-digit PIN so only you can read it.

---

## Lock notes with PIN

- **Lock any note** with a 4-digit PIN; the note’s content is encrypted before it’s saved.
- **Unlock** when you need to view or edit; lock again or leave the app to re-protect.
- **Change PIN** or **Remove lock** from the note’s More (⋯) menu—you’ll confirm your current PIN when changing or removing.
- Locked notes can’t be shared via link until you remove the lock.

---

## Tips

- **Set a PIN you’ll remember**—if you forget it, the note content cannot be recovered.
- Use **Lock** from the note’s More menu to lock again after unlocking (without closing the app).
- For more, see **Help** in the app or the `/help` folder in the repo.
