# What's New in Harvous — November 5, 2026

**Release Date:** November 5, 2026
**Version:** v3.29.4

Two small fixes for reading and writing on your phone.

---

## Updates in This Release

### Scripture cards keep their title

**What changed:**
- An open Scripture card on your phone now shows the book, verses and translation at the top, the same as when it is collapsed

**How it helps you:**
- You can always see which passage you are reading

### A cleaner edge on iPhone

**What changed:**
- Notes and Bible chapters now curve at the bottom corners of the screen instead of being cut on a diagonal
- The soft shadow around the edge of the app is gone on phones

**How it helps you:**
- The page sits cleanly in your screen, with no blurred band around it
