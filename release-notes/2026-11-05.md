# What's New in Harvous — November 5, 2026

**Release Date:** November 5, 2026
**Version:** v3.31.0

Today's passage gets the day's sky, and a few fixes for reading and writing on your phone.

---

## Updates in This Release

### Today's passage, under today's sky

**What changed:**
- Today's passage on Home now opens on a painting of the sky at that hour: dawn in the morning, a bright sky through the day, sunset in the evening, and stars at night
- The passage reference is set large over the sky, and the words begin as the sky fades into the page

**How it helps you:**
- The day's passage feels like the start of the day, not one more box on the page

### Scripture cards keep their title

**What changed:**
- An open Scripture card on your phone now shows the book, verses and translation at the top, the same as when it is collapsed

**How it helps you:**
- You can always see which passage you are reading

### A cleaner edge on iPhone

**What changed:**
- Notes and Bible chapters now curve at the bottom corners of the screen instead of being cut on a diagonal
- The soft shadow around the edge of the app is gone on phones

**How it helps you:**
- The page sits cleanly in your screen, with no blurred band around it

### Home sits cleanly in your screen

**What changed:**
- On phones, the bottom of your Home page now curves along with your screen's corners, with nothing cut off on a straight line

**How it helps you:**
- The last thing on the page fades out neatly instead of being sliced
