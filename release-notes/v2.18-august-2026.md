# What's New in Harvous v2.18.0

**Release Date:** August 4, 2026
**Version:** 2.18.0

This release brings Harvous to churches, and makes moving around the sidebar quicker and steadier.

---

## Updates in This Release

### Harvous for churches

Churches can now publish study to the people who attend them, and those people can follow along in their own Harvous.

**What changed:**

- Churches publish study through ministry channels — one channel per ministry or study context, such as adult education, students, or a sermon series companion
- Once you pick your church in Settings, you can browse the channels it publishes and follow the ones you want
- Study from the channels you follow appears on Home under "From your church"
- Anything your church publishes stays read-only, and you can start your own note from it whenever something is worth keeping in your own words
- Church staff can invite and manage their own team, and set up channels themselves
- Churches can start on a pilot and subscribe when they are ready

**How it helps you:**

- Your church's teaching and your own notes live in the same place
- You choose what you follow, and you can stop following at any time
- What you write stays yours — following a church never gives anyone access to your notes

### A quicker sidebar

**What changed:**

- The space menu now lists everything in one place: My Home, your church, and the spaces inside each of them
- Any space is one click away, including a church channel while you are looking at My Home
- Everything in the menu lines up in a single column, so it reads as one list instead of a staircase
- Switching between the space view and the list view is still a single click on either button
- A long church name now stays on one line and slides to reveal itself when you hover

**How it helps you:**

- Fewer clicks to get where you are going
- The menu is easier to scan at a glance
- Nothing shifts around as you move between spaces

---

## Tips for Using New Features

- **Set your church:** Go to Settings, then My Church, and pick your church from the directory
- **Follow what you want:** Open My Church to see the channels your church publishes, and follow the ones that fit
- **Make it yours:** On a note from your church, use "Save a copy" to start your own version
