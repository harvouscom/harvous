# What's New in Harvous — August 6, 2026

**Release Date:** August 6, 2026
**Versions:** v2.21.0–v2.33.0

The day Harvous for churches went out, and then a long afternoon of making the Planner worth using. The full story of the church release is in [Harvous for churches](launches/harvous-for-churches.md) — this is the day's own record.

---

## Updates in This Release

### The Planner, at full size

**What changed:**

- The Planner opens across the whole pane instead of the sidebar column
- Three ways to look at the same plan: a board you drag weeks around on, a calendar, and a list
- The sermon editor is a rail down the side now, not a window over the top
- An idea with no date yet sits in a strip under the plan, waiting for one

**How it helps you:**

- You can see the quarter you are planning while you edit a week of it
- Dragging a sermon to another Sunday is one motion, rather than opening it, changing the date and saving

### A series knows how long it runs

**What changed:**

- Set how many weeks a series runs, and the weeks you have not written yet appear as empty slots
- A series tells you how many of its weeks are still to write
- An unwritten week is marked the same way everywhere it appears, in the board, the calendar and the list
- Renaming a series renames it everywhere it appears

**How it helps you:**

- A six-week series looks like six weeks from the day you plan it, not like one week that keeps growing
- You can see at a glance what is left to write, instead of counting

### A shelf for your church

**What changed:**

- Your church can keep the links and files it teaches from in one place, with staff choosing who each resource is for
- A resource can belong to the whole church, or to particular rooms, like Youth or the Tuesday study
- Anyone connected to your church can suggest a resource, and a pastor or admin decides what gets added
- Anything on the shelf can be attached to a planned sermon, from either size of the editor

**How it helps you:**

- The commentary your pastor keeps recommending is somewhere you can actually find it
- Your church's shelf is shaped by the people using it, not only by staff

### Reading the plan at a glance

**What changed:**

- The board's future weeks show their dates, and the week you are in stands out instead of the ones you are not
- Calendar entries are sized for a day rather than for a board column
- Empty panes say what belongs there and offer the way out, rather than showing a bare line of text
- The sermon editor's actions stay at the bottom of the rail instead of drifting up the page

**How it helps you:**

- The plan reads at a glance instead of asking you to work out which week is which
- A pane you have not filled yet tells you what it is for

### Faster and easier to tap

**What changed:**

- Every pane under My Church shows a loading indicator while it is fetching, and the Planner opens noticeably faster
- The sidebar opens and closes faster
- Menu rows are bigger, which matters most on a phone
- Choice controls use the app's own check orb rather than the browser's dropdown

**How it helps you:**

- You can tell the difference between "still loading" and "there is nothing here"
- Opening the Planner no longer feels like waiting
