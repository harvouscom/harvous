# What's New in Harvous — January 24, 2026

**Release Date:** January 24, 2026
**Versions:** v1.36.0–v1.39.0

## Major Feature: Share Your Threads and Notes!

This is a big one! Harvous v1.39.0 brings **sharing functionality** to threads and notes, making it easier than ever to share your Bible study insights with others and help grow the Harvous community.

---

## New Sharing Features

### Share Your Notes

**What you can do:**
- Click the "Share" button on any note to create a unique, shareable link
- Share your insights with anyone—even if they don't have a Harvous account yet
- Recipients see a beautiful preview of your note
- When they join Harvous, the note automatically appears in their inbox

**How it helps:**
- Share your favorite insights on social media, in messages, or anywhere
- Help others discover meaningful Bible study content
- Grow the Harvous community by sharing what you're learning

### Share Your Threads

**What you can do:**
- Share entire threads of connected notes and insights
- Create public links that anyone can view
- Recipients can see all the notes in your thread in a beautiful, organized view
- When they join, they can add the entire thread to their Harvous

**How it helps:**
- Share complete study series or topics you've been working through
- Help others discover organized, thoughtful Bible study content
- Show how you connect different insights together

### How Sharing Works

1. **Click "Share"** on any note or thread
2. **Get a unique link** that you can share anywhere
3. **Recipients see a preview** of your content (even without a Harvous account)
4. **They can join Harvous** to add it to their own study collection
5. **Content appears in their inbox** when they sign up

## Why This Matters

Sharing is about more than just convenience—it's about **growing the Harvous community** and helping others discover meaningful Bible study. When you share:

- **You help others** discover insights they might not have found
- **You grow the community** by introducing new people to Harvous
- **You create connections** around shared study and learning

## Getting Started

**To share a note:**
1. Open any note
2. Click the "Share" button (usually in the note menu or toolbar)
3. Copy the link and share it anywhere!

**To share a thread:**
1. Open any thread
2. Click "Share" in the thread options
3. Share the link with others!
