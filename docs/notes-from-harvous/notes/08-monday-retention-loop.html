If Sunday fades by Wednesday, the answer isn’t guilt. It’s a small loop.

Four moves:

  1. Capture (Sun night / Mon morning, 5 min) — passage, one sentence you don’t want to lose, one open question.
  2. Retrieve (Mon/Tue, 3 min) — say the sentence without looking; then check. Blanking is useful data.
  3. Relocate (midweek, 5–10 min) — put it where life happens: calendar nudge, friend text, five verses on the train.
  4. Re-enter (before next gathering, 5 min) — open last week’s note before taking in something new.

Why it works: people remember what they practice retrieving, not only what they once heard.

Teachers: capture what the room wrestled with. Group leaders: relocate can be one midweek question—not a worksheet.

Refuse loops that need a perfect morning routine or spiritual scorekeeping. Shrink until it fits real life. Run it three Mondays before you judge it.
