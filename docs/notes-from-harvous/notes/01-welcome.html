Welcome. This thread is a short working library for teachers, group leaders, and anyone who wants Sunday’s teaching to last into the week.

How to use it: Read in order the first time if you can. Jump around later. These notes are compressed companions to longer essays on the Harvous blog—same ideas, smaller footprint.

What this is: Practice notes about how the church teaches and how learning lasts. Tools help; humans teach. The gathered body still matters more than any app.

What this isn’t: A curriculum quarter, a counseling substitute, or a content mill. If you’re in crisis, please reach out to a trusted professional or a local crisis line.
