A sermon series can give a church a shared text and vocabulary. Then small group gets awkward: either people recreate the sermon (badly), or they ignore it for a random question sheet from another universe.

Third way: follow the same trail without repeating the same talk.

Midweek is for retrieving what was heard, asking what the sanctuary didn’t have time for, and applying the text among people who know each other’s weeks. Sunday was exposure; midweek can be return—see retrieval practice.

Bridge note (four lines): passage with verses; one-sentence weekly aim; two questions (what did you hear? / where does this meet us?); one open edge.

Simple shape: arrive → retrieve without looking → re-open the text aloud → honest edges → one sentence to carry.

People will miss Sundays. Keep the bridge linked to the passage. Offer a sixty-second “so far”—not every illustration. The text stays the center.

Pastors: a published one-sentence aim helps leaders. Leaders: follow up the text; take concerns to the preacher like a teammate.
