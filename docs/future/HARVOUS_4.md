# Harvous 4

> **Status:** thinking-out-loud, not a ship date. Written so the four products stay in one picture before any of them get built in isolation.
>
> **Related:** [REVIEWS_CHALLENGES_SEASON_PASS_STRATEGY.md](./REVIEWS_CHALLENGES_SEASON_PASS_STRATEGY.md) (canonical Review / Challenges / Season Pass thesis), [CHALLENGES_AS_SUGGESTIONS.md](./CHALLENGES_AS_SUGGESTIONS.md) (why personal Challenges was withheld), [MONETIZATION_AND_PRICING.md](./MONETIZATION_AND_PRICING.md), [CHURCH_ORG_AND_CURRICULUM.md](./CHURCH_ORG_AND_CURRICULUM.md), [STUDY_PLANS.md](./STUDY_PLANS.md), [HARVOUS_SDK_AND_FUTURE_ROADMAP.md](./HARVOUS_SDK_AND_FUTURE_ROADMAP.md).

Four plans. One home for Bible study that remembers.

## Why four

Harvous 3 already has the loop: write a note, keep Scripture in the flow, thread what belongs together, let Suggestions bring a fading thought back. Plus already unlocks Review and hosting Shared Spaces. Challenges is already named on the public roadmap as time-boxed work, solo or with others.

Harvous 4 is not a fifth product. It is the moment those pieces become four clear plans people can name — and the moment Review stops being a private drill and becomes a season the church and the public can walk through together.

The four plans are **Personal** (free), **Plus**, **Church**, and **Challenges**. Personal stays the home for a single student’s notes. Plus stays the paid layer for Review and hosting rooms. Church is the plan that treats a congregation as a first-class host. Challenges is the open season — public by default, hosted in shared spaces, optionally hosted by a church, and built so Review becomes a curated test instead of a lone quiz.

## The four plans

| Plan | Who it is for | What they get | What they do not lose |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Personal** | Anyone studying the Bible. No card. | Unlimited notes, pills, highlights, threads, @ mentions, reminders, Daily Passage, joining spaces. | The whole core app. Challenges seasons they can join. Study guides from seasons they finish. |
| **Plus** | The student who wants Review and rooms of their own. | Review. Host Shared Spaces (cover, threads, up to 50). Deeper recall history. | Everything in Personal. Can run a small group challenge inside a space they host. |
| **Church** | A congregation, campus, or ministry that wants to host study at scale. | Church Space(s), member roles, seasonal Challenge hosting, branded cover, staff Review packs, reporting that stays pastoral — not surveillance. | Members keep Personal (or Plus) homes. Church never swallows private notes. |
| **Challenges** | The public. Existing users. Churches that want a season with a finish line. | Open seasons, shared-space rooms, curated tests built on Review, a study guide that survives the season. | Joining is free. Hosting a public season may sit on Plus or Church. Your private notes stay yours. |

## Personal — still free

Personal is the promise that must not move. Unlimited notes. Scripture pills in the translations Harvous already carries. Highlights and annotations. Folders that file themselves and threads you connect by hand. @ mentions. Reminders. Daily Passage. Search. Offline and sync. Joining a Shared Space is always free.

In Harvous 4, Personal also means: you can enter a public Challenge, write inside the season’s shared space, sit the curated Review tests for that season, and keep the study guide when the season ends. You should not need Plus to practice. Plus is for hosting and for Review that is not bound to a public season.

- **Guardrail:** no artificial cap that makes the free home feel like a trial of the paid one.
- **Guardrail:** private notes never leak into a Challenge space unless the writer places them there.
- **Guardrail:** a finished Challenge study guide lives in the person’s own library, not only in the season room.

## Plus — Review and rooms

Plus stays the individual paid plan. The current shape is already right: Review (write an answer from memory, then say how it went, from your own notes and verses) and hosting Shared Spaces — unlimited spaces, up to 50 people each, joining always free.

Harvous 4 should deepen Plus without turning it into Church-lite:

- **Own-note Review packs.** Review packs you build from your own threads — a set of prompts that follow a book, a topic, or a sermon series you already wrote through.
- **Host a private season.** A Plus user can host a closed Challenge inside a space they own: same Review engine, smaller room, not listed in the public season catalog.
- **Recall history that is yours.** How a note, highlight, or verse came back; what you marked easy or hard. Useful later when a public Challenge builds a test from similar material.

Pricing can stay in the neighborhood of today’s Plus ($6/mo or $36/yr founding). Church should not be a Plus multiplier sold to one person. It is a different customer.

## Church — the congregation as host

Church is the missing named plan. Shared Spaces already let a group share a room. Church is what happens when that room is a ministry, not a friend group: more than one space, roles that match real church life (pastor / teacher / group leader / member / guest), a cover that looks like the church rather than a generic room, and the ability to host a seasonal Challenge for the body — or to adopt a public Challenge as “ours this quarter.”

### What Church is for

- Sermon series that leave a trail in a shared space instead of disappearing into fifty Apple Notes.
- Small groups that pick up next week where this week stopped.
- A church-wide Challenge (Lent, Advent, “the Gospel of John this fall”) with a curated Review test and a study guide the whole body can keep.
- Staff who need to prepare Review prompts from the series, not from a stranger’s public pack.

### What Church is not

- Not a church management system. No giving, check-in, or membership database. Harvous remains a notes home.
- Not a surveillance product. Pastors see participation at the level a good small-group leader would — who joined the season, who finished a Review set — not the private notes in a member’s Personal space.
- Not a content marketplace that replaces the pastor’s voice. The church writes or adopts the season. Harvous supplies the room, the Review engine, and the study-guide output.

### Membership and notes

Every church member still has a Personal home. The Church Space is a shared drawer, not a replacement drawer. A note can live in both when the writer threads it that way. Default is private. Share is a choice.

## Challenges — the fourth product

Challenges is the plan that celebrates the other three at once. It is open to the public. It uses Shared Spaces as the room. A church can host one. And it takes Review from a private practice into a seasonal, curated test — with a study guide that existing Harvous users keep when the season closes.

This is the public face of what [REVIEWS_CHALLENGES_SEASON_PASS_STRATEGY.md](./REVIEWS_CHALLENGES_SEASON_PASS_STRATEGY.md) called Season Pass, without requiring a separate consumer SKU on day one. Joining stays free. The paid acts are *hosting* (Plus or Church) and *unbounded personal Review* (Plus).

### The job of a Challenge

A Challenge is a time-boxed season with a start, a path, and a finish. Not a streak counter. Not a reading plan that forgets you wrote anything. The path is study. The finish is Review you can actually sit — questions drawn from the season’s passages, the host’s notes and prompts, and (if you opt in) the notes you yourself filed during the weeks.

### How a season is built

- **Host.** A public catalog season (Harvous-hosted or church-hosted but open) or a closed season inside a Plus or Church space.
- **Curriculum.** Passages, a suggested weekly rhythm, host-written threads, optional templates (lesson, outline, sermon notes). Prefer existing study-plan sequences (`STUDY_PLANS.md`) over a new content type.
- **Room.** The Shared Space for the season. Joining is free. Writing in the space is writing in Harvous — pills, highlights, threads work the same.
- **Curated test.** A Review set that grows as the season unfolds. Early weeks are recall of this week’s text. Later weeks mix earlier weeks. The final sitting is the “test” — still in the Review voice: write from memory, then say how it went.
- **Study guide.** When the season ends, every participant can keep a Study Guide generated from the season: the passage map, the host prompts, their own answers and notes they chose to include, and the Review items they missed and may want again.

### Why this is the advantage for existing users

People who already live in Harvous do not need another app for a 40-day plan. They need the season to practice the thing they already came here to do: remember Scripture and their own study. Challenges give each season a study guide to practice and learn from — Review with a curriculum and a finish line, not Review as an empty box.

That is the lift Review has been waiting for. Review on Plus is “quiz yourself from what you happened to write.” Challenges is “this season chose the text; you wrote through it; now sit with it; then keep the guide.”

### Public and church, together

A Challenge can be listed publicly so anyone with Personal can join. A Church plan holder can host that listing, or clone it into a members-only room. A Plus user can run a quiet version for a group of twelve. Same engine. Three doors.

### Tone

Challenges should feel like a well-taught class, not a leaderboard. Optional cohort presence (who else is in this week’s sitting) is enough. Rankings, public scores, and shame mechanics stay out — consistent with the 3.0 promise of no score, no streak, and no leaderboard. The glory is searching the matter — not beating the person next to you.

## How the four plans touch each other

Think of one object model, four doors:

- Note, highlight, thread, folder, space — unchanged from Harvous 3.
- Review item — already Plus. In 4, a Review item can belong to a person, to a space, or to a Challenge season.
- Season — new. Has dates, a space, a prompt set, a study-guide template.
- Study guide — new artifact. A note-like document (or a thread of them) that is generated at milestones and at the close, owned by the participant.

Personal can consume seasons and keep guides. Plus can host small seasons and build Review from private notes. Church can host large seasons and staff the prompts. Challenges is the public face of seasons — and the reason a study guide exists as a first-class thing.

## What we will not do in 4

- Replace the Bible reader people already love. Harvous stays the notes home beside it.
- Center AI-written devotionals. Connector can wait. Challenges are curated by hosts, not generated as filler.
- Turn Church into ChMS.
- Paywall the act of joining a public Challenge or keeping the study guide from a season you finished.
- Show pastors a member’s Personal notes.
- Ship native Apple apps as a dependency of 4. They can land on their own track.

## A possible sequence, not a calendar

This is order of learning, not a promise of quarters.

1. Name the four plans in public language. Personal / Plus / Church / Challenges. Even if Church is “coming,” the map should be visible.
2. Make Review items attachable to a space, not only to a person. That is the bridge from Plus Review to a hosted test.
3. Ship a single Harvous-hosted public Challenge (one book, 4–6 weeks) as the prototype. Measure: join rate from Personal, finish rate on the final sitting, whether people open the study guide after week one.
4. Let Plus hosts clone that prototype into a private space.
5. Open Church as a plan when a real congregation wants to host the next season — not before. Design roles with that first church, not in the abstract.
6. Only then build the catalog: upcoming seasons, past study guides, “adopt this as our church’s fall.”

## Success, said plainly

Harvous 4 works if a person who already takes notes can point to a season and say: I practiced, I was tested on what I actually studied, and I still have the guide. It works if a church can host that season without becoming a software project. It works if Personal is still free and still enough to study every day.

Four plans. The same home. A season that makes Review matter to more than the person who already paid for Plus.

Keep your Bible app. Add Harvous. In 4, add a season worth finishing.
