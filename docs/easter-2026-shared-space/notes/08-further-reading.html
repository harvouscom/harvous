One practical next step: read 1 Corinthians 15:1–58 in a translation you find readable, then revisit the note Why Jesus’ resurrection matters. If you want narrative context first, Mark 14:1–72, Mark 15:1–47, and Mark 16:1–20—or Luke 22:1–71, Luke 23:1–56, and Luke 24:1–53—pair well with that chapter. Romans 10:9 is a single verse many people use to summarize the resurrection’s place in early Christian confession—short enough to memorize if you like that sort of habit.
