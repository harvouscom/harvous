We’re glad you’re here. Whether you’re new to Jesus or you’ve walked with Him a long time, there’s room in this thread—we’re slowing down for Easter together: what the story is, why it matters, and how people think carefully about the claims. That’s what we mean by hope for every heart here—not a slogan, an honest invitation.

How to use this thread: Read the notes in order the first time if you can; jump around later if that fits you better. Add your own notes or questions when your group is ready.

Ground rules: Be kind. It’s okay to say “I don’t know yet.” Honest questions are welcome. We’re not here to attack people or win arguments—we’re here to learn and worship with humility. The psalmist’s picture of God drawing near to the brokenhearted in Psalm 34:18 is a good tone to borrow, even when we disagree.

Important: This is a study conversation, not counseling or medical advice. If you’re in crisis, please reach out to a trusted professional or a crisis line where you live.
